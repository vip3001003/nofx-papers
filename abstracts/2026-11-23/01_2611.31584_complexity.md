# Absence of $σ$-criticality in Short-Range Spin Glasses

- **arXiv**: [2611.31584](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31584)
- **Date**: 2026-11-23
- **Category**: complexity
- **Authors**: C. M. Newman, D. L. Stein
- **NOFX Relevance Score**: 1

## Abstract

The stability of ground states in the Edwards-Anderson Ising spin glass in dimensions two and higher against perturbations of a single coupling can be quantified through the ``flexibility'' of that coupling in a particular ground state. The property of $σ$-criticality is one in which varying a single coupling can alter the flexibilities of a (strictly) positive density of couplings in an infinite system, and its presence provides a sufficient condition for the presence of multiple ground state pairs. In a previous paper it was shown for the EA spin glass that $σ$-criticality of the first kind, in which the critical droplet of a bond has a positive-density interface with the ground state, was ruled out in any dimension. The remaining possibility, called $σ$-criticality of the second kind, is one in which a bond lies in the critical droplet boundaries of a positive density of other bonds. We show here that this kind of $σ$-criticality is also absent, ruling out altogether the presence of $σ$-criticality in the EA spin glass. While this doesn't resolve the question of multiplicity of ground state pairs, it removes a sufficient condition for its presence. We then propose a rigorous necessary condition for ground state pair multiplicity.
