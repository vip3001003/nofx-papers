# Eigenvalue-based computation of Richardson pairing spectra and finite-size thermodynamics

- **arXiv**: [2611.31531](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31531)
- **Date**: 2026-11-23
- **Category**: complexity
- **Authors**: Grzegorz Biskowski, Marcin R. Piatek, Franco Ferrari
- **NOFX Relevance Score**: 1

## Abstract

We present a numerical workflow for the reduced BCS Richardson model, combining the eigenvalue-based Bethe equations, Baxter-polynomial reconstruction, and complex root finding. Newton-Raphson iteration solves the nonlinear equations for the eigenvalue-based variables; a linear system yields the polynomial coefficients, and Laguerre iteration with polynomial deflation extracts the rapidities. We apply the procedure to equally spaced, quadratic, and hydrogen-like non-degenerate single-particle spectra as the pairing strength varies. For finite systems, we calculate canonical observables from the complete seniority-zero spectrum and check benchmark spectra against exact diagonalization. In an eight-level, four-pair system with a hydrogen-like spectrum, the heat capacity has two maxima at weak coupling. With increasing coupling, the low-temperature maximum shifts upward and the high-temperature maximum downward; the latter ceases to be a distinct local maximum near $g\simeq0.03515$ in the units used. The equally spaced benchmark instead has a single dominant maximum over the coupling interval displayed for that spectrum. This comparison illustrates how single-particle level spacings can shape finite-size thermal response. The C++ code is publicly available; numerical instabilities remain possible near ill-conditioned configurations and at larger sizes.
