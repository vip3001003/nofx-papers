# Localization in one-dimensional magnetic quasicrystals

- **arXiv**: [2611.31384](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31384)
- **Date**: 2026-11-23
- **Category**: complexity
- **Authors**: Andrew Osborne, Rhine Samajdar, Andrew A. Houck
- **NOFX Relevance Score**: 1

## Abstract

The celebrated Aubry-André-Harper model undergoes a metal-insulator transition akin to Anderson localization due to an energetic competition between nearest-neighbor hopping and a quasiperiodic on-site potential. In this work, we consider the problem of a one-dimensional lattice subject to a quasiperiodic external field, where the topology of the underlying graph mediates the competition between insulating and conducting phases. In particular, we present examples for which all dispersive eigenstates are critically localized, as well as models in which they are exponentially localized. We also demonstrate the coexistence of these phenomena with an extensive set of degenerate finitely supported eigenstates forming a flat band. Finally, we construct models with exactly solvable mobility edges arising only from graph structure. Going beyond these single-particle results, we show---using exact-diagonalization calculations of the bosonic dynamics---that, as in the conventional Aubry-André-Harper case, on-site interactions drive localized eigenstates toward extendedness. Our results provide a framework for exploring how magnetic interference and interactions jointly constrain transport in quasiperiodic lattices.
