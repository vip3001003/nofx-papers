# Third-Order Asymptotics for Fixed-Length Lossy Compression of Discrete Memoryless Sources

- **arXiv**: [2611.13573](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13573)
- **Date**: 2026-11-10
- **Category**: info_theory
- **Authors**: Vincent Y. F. Tan
- **NOFX Relevance Score**: 0

## Abstract

This paper determines the third-order asymptotics of the logarithm of the minimum codebook size for fixed-length lossy compression of discrete memoryless sources. For an excess-distortion probability constraint $\varepsilon\in(0,1)$ and every interior distortion level, we establish a third-order correction of $\frac12\log n+O(1)$, where $n$ is the blocklength. This sharpens the general bounds of Kostina and Verdú [IEEE Trans. Inf. Theory, 2012]. The main technical innovation lies in the converse for positive dispersion. Under a reverse change of measure, we retain the exponential weights associated with tilted information and distortion slack and control them jointly using the multivariate weighted-strip estimate of Scarlett, Martinez, and Guillén i Fàbregas [Proc. IEEE ISIT, 2015]. A pairing argument verifies the required covariance nondegeneracy uniformly over all reconstruction words whose distortion balls meet a typical set, yielding the matching converse.
