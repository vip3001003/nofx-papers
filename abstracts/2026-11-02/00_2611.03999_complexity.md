# Maximum entropy explanation for the ubiquity of power-law mechanical responses

- **arXiv**: [2611.03999](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03999)
- **Date**: 2026-11-02
- **Category**: complexity
- **Authors**: Sverre Holm
- **NOFX Relevance Score**: 0

## Abstract

The ubiquitous power-law relaxation emerges from complex mechanical systems with a vast number of interacting exponential responses. Here we demonstrate that power-law responses satisfy a maximum entropy criterion, regulating the individual contributions under partial knowledge. This leads to a prediction that frequency range of validity decreases with power-law exponent, a property which is confirmed for some biological materials. The close relation with the fractional standard linear solid also leads to an incorporation of the equally ubiquitous stretched exponential behavior.
