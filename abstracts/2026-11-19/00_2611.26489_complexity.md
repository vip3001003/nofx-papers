# Nonstandard quantum statistics for strongly correlated systems: Superexclusion in momentum space and comparison with exact results

- **arXiv**: [2611.26489](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26489)
- **Date**: 2026-11-19
- **Category**: complexity
- **Authors**: Piotr Kuterba, Mateusz Wójcik, Józef Spałek
- **NOFX Relevance Score**: 0

## Abstract

We analyze in detail nonstandard statistics for strongly correlated fermions, which comprise the statistical spin liquid (SSL) and model non-Fermi liquid (NFL) cases. Both of those cases are based on the total exclusion of local doubly occupancies ($the~superexclusion$) either in real space (SSL case) or in reciprocal ($\mathbf{k}$) space (NFL). The results obtained on a purely physical ground are supplemented with the exact analysis of those coming from microscopic Hubbard-like model in $\mathbf{k}$ space. Specifically, we discuss the transmutation of those statistics to that of localized magnetic moments, as well as the transformation of the Fermi liquid into a strongly correlated fermionic liquid or Mott insulating state; the latter taking place for the half-filled-band situation. The role of exclusion of double occupancies in either real or reciprocal space in the appearance of the correlated phase is stressed. Conclusions concerning the universal features of the models, independence of either the real- or $\mathbf{k}$-space representation, are also discussed. One of those is the boson to fermion statistics transmutation which in the limit of strong repulsive interboson interaction leads to the particle localization instead to the Bose-Einstein condensation.
