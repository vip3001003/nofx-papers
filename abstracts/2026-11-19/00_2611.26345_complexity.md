# First-passage and correlated reset-count statistics under threshold and Poissonian resetting in a two-dimensional wedge geometry

- **arXiv**: [2611.26345](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26345)
- **Date**: 2026-11-19
- **Category**: complexity
- **Authors**: Fazil Najeeb, Arnab Pal, V. V Prasad
- **NOFX Relevance Score**: 0

## Abstract

We study the interplay between threshold resetting (TR) and Poissonian resetting in a Brownian target search problem confined to a two-dimensional open wedge of apex angle $α$. The boundary at zero angle acts as an absorbing target, while encounters with the boundary at $α$ trigger TR, instantaneously restarting the search from its initial position. For TR alone, using the renewal framework developed by Biswas et al. in Phys. Rev. Lett. 135, 227101 (2025), we obtain exact first and second moments of the first-passage time (FPT), whose density exhibits an algebraic long-time tail $t^{-1-\fracπ{2α}}$. To combine the two resetting mechanisms, we thereafter develop a generic random-variable-based renewal formalism to derive the combined mean FPT. We show that the coefficient of variation of the underlying TR dynamics determines whether Poissonian resetting accelerates or hinders the search for $0<α<π/4$, whereas for $α\geqπ/4$, Poissonian resetting always reduces the MFPT. Furthermore, we derive the joint count statistics of the two resetting mechanisms and characterize their mutual dependence through Pearson's correlation coefficient. Interestingly, we uncover a crossover from weak to strong correlations as the Poissonian resetting rate is varied, with the crossover behavior exhibiting a nontrivial dependence on the initial search position. Our framework provides a basis for studying competing resetting mechanisms in more general geometries.
