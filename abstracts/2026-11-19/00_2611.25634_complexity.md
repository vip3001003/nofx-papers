# Two Faces of Spacetime Density Matrices: Entanglement and Complexity

- **arXiv**: [2611.25634](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25634)
- **Date**: 2026-11-19
- **Category**: complexity
- **Authors**: Wu-zhong Guo
- **NOFX Relevance Score**: 0

## Abstract

We establish a direct connection between entanglement and complexity through spacetime density matrices. For stationary pure states and complementary regions at different times, spacetime replica moments encode the return amplitude of a replica-defect quench. This representation defines an associated state spread complexity, which we call Krylov-Rényi complexity. It can equivalently be obtained by using a replica-defect excitation of the replicated background state as the seed and evolving it under the original replicated Hamiltonian. We further construct Krylov-von Neumann complexity as a probe limit from first-order replica data. Timelike entanglement and complexity provide complementary information at a fixed time, while the full time dependence of the timelike entropy determines the Krylov dynamics and hence the complexity. The replica construction fixes the seed and its evolution through the background state, subsystem geometry, and regulator, without an independently chosen probe operator. Like entanglement entropy, the resulting complexity is defined nonperturbatively and probes universal properties and dynamical information about the underlying theory.
