# Wigner-Dyson correlations in selected sectors of an integrable spin chain

- **arXiv**: [2611.26263](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26263)
- **Date**: 2026-11-19
- **Category**: complexity
- **Authors**: Balázs Pozsgay
- **NOFX Relevance Score**: 0

## Abstract

In quantum many-body physics spectral statistics is often used as a probe to distinguish integrable and chaotic models. We find a special case of an integrable spin chain with unusual spectral properties. With open boundary conditions the model has homogeneous exponential degeneracies; these are split for periodic boundary conditions. In the latter case, we find that a small number of sectors (selected by a global symmetry) showcase Wigner-Dyson spectral statistics, which usually signals quantum chaos. Our model is clean, translationally invariant, and it fits into the standard Yang-Baxter framework. We identify Hilbert space fragmentation as the key mechanism behind the spectral properties. The fragmentation is non-local, and its extent is different in the case of open and periodic boundary conditions. We argue that the dynamics of the model is not chaotic despite the unusual spectral correlations.
