# Optimal Min-Entropy Estimation with High Probability

- **arXiv**: [2611.23116](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23116)
- **Date**: 2026-11-17
- **Category**: info_theory
- **Authors**: Qisheng Wang
- **NOFX Relevance Score**: 0

## Abstract

Min-entropy plays a fundamental role in assessing the unpredictability of randomness sources. In particular, NIST SP 800-90B specifies min-entropy estimation as a key procedure for entropy sources used in cryptographic random bit generators, illustrating the practical importance of reliable min-entropy estimation. In this paper, we settle the sample complexity of min-entropy estimation with high probability in both the classical and quantum cases. Specifically, for min-entropy estimation with additive error $\varepsilon$ and probability at least $1-δ$, the sample complexity is $Θ(d\log(d/δ)/\varepsilon^2)$ for classical distributions on $d$ symbols and $Θ((d^2+d\log(1/δ))/\varepsilon^2)$ for quantum states on a $d$-dimensional Hilbert space. Our results admit short and simple proofs.
