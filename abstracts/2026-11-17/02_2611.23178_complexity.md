# Photon condensation driven by scalar spin chirality

- **arXiv**: [2611.23178](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23178)
- **Date**: 2026-11-17
- **Category**: complexity
- **Authors**: Dimitrije Stepanenko, Daniel Loss, Mircea Trif
- **NOFX Relevance Score**: 2

## Abstract

Gauge invariance forbids equilibrium photon condensation for spatially uniform electric-dipole coupling, motivating alternative routes based on orbital magnetic response. We show that triangular molecular magnets coupled to a cavity support photon condensation driven by emergent scalar spin chirality. Starting from the half-filled Hubbard model in the Mott regime, we derive an effective Hamiltonian in which virtual ring exchange couples the cavity flux directly to scalar spin chirality. Mean-field theory, collective-fluctuation calculations, and finite-size exact diagonalization reveal a phase with spontaneous chirality ordering and a macroscopic cavity displacement, together with hybrid chirality-photon excitations. The condensation threshold can be tuned electrically through the molecular chirality splitting. Our results establish emergent orbital degrees of freedom in frustrated magnets as a route to equilibrium light-matter ordering beyond conventional dipolar cavity quantum electrodynamics.
