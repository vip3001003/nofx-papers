# Dynamical theory of the East model. II. A path-probability formalism

- **arXiv**: [2611.23008](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23008)
- **Date**: 2026-11-17
- **Category**: complexity
- **Authors**: Chong Zha, Yuliang Jin
- **NOFX Relevance Score**: 0

## Abstract

We develop a dynamical theory of the East model---a one-dimensional, hierarchically constrained kinetic Ising model---based on path-probability densities. This theory yields an infinite but closed set of equations for single-spin path-probability densities, which, after Laplace transformation, become algebraic difference equations. To render the theory tractable, we introduce a systematic factorization approximation in which the joint probability densities of consecutive flip intervals are factorized into lower-order joint probability densities. We implement the first-order single-interval factorization approximation, obtain a closed set of four equations for the persistence functions, and solve them numerically. The resulting time correlation functions and relaxation times agree closely with Monte Carlo simulations and with the two-domain-basis approximation of the Zwanzig--Mori formalism over a wide range of temperatures, although both approximations fail to capture the exact low-temperature asymptotic super-Arrhenius scaling of the relaxation time. The present formalism provides a plausible foundation for a complete and rigorous theoretical description of the dynamics of the East model and offers a promising route toward generalization to other systems.
