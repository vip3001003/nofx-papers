# Topological signatures of criticality: the Ising case

- **arXiv**: [2611.23586](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23586)
- **Date**: 2026-11-17
- **Category**: complexity
- **Authors**: Tak-Shing Chan, Paweł Dłotko, Jacek Gulgowski et al.
- **NOFX Relevance Score**: 3

## Abstract

Topological properties of microscopic configurations may provide additional insight into phase transitions beyond conventional thermodynamic observables. We investigate this idea in the two-dimensional Ising model on square and triangular lattices by associating cell complexes with the positive and negative spin domains and constructing an Euler-characteristic observable from their difference. We provide the expression relating this quantity to conventional observables of the Ising model. This derivation allows us to understand its expected behavior physically. As a byproduct, we show that at any finite positive temperature the expectation of the Euler characteristic exceeds a positive, lattice-size-independent bound, hence disproving a previously suggested conjecture. Monte Carlo simulations combined with multihistogram reweighting show that this spin-odd topological observable exhibits order-parameter-like behavior near the ferromagnetic transition. Finite-size scaling of its susceptibility yields pseudocritical temperatures that converge to the known critical temperature for both lattice geometries. On the square lattice, the leading-order estimate of the critical exponent obtained from the topological observable depends more strongly on the fitting range than the magnetization estimate. This behavior is consistent with stronger subleading finite-size corrections, as supported by fits including an additional correction term. On the triangular lattice, the corresponding estimate remains consistent with the theoretical Ising value. Our study suggests that topologically defined quantities such as the Euler characteristic can provide an alternative way to locate order-disorder phase transitions in the systems considered here. Since the definition of these observables is purely geometrical, they can provide useful insights in cases in which order parameters cannot be constructed.
