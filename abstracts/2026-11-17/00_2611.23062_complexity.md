# Unique continuation for the Pauli-Fierz Hamiltonian

- **arXiv**: [2611.23062](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23062)
- **Date**: 2026-11-17
- **Category**: complexity
- **Authors**: Mihály A. Csirik
- **NOFX Relevance Score**: 0

## Abstract

We prove a unique continuation property for eigenstates of the Pauli-Fierz Hamiltonian, viewed as functions of the electron configuration with values in the spin-photon space. The vector potential may lie in $L^6_\mathrm{loc}$, with its divergence, curl, and the scalar potential in $L^p_\mathrm{loc}$ for some $p>2$. As an application, we prove a Hohenberg-Kohn theorem at positive coupling strength: the spin-summed ground-state density and combined current density determine the external potentials up to a gauge transformation and an additive constant.
