# A Universal Algebraic Construction of Hilbert-Space Fragmentation

- **arXiv**: [2611.19470](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.19470)
- **Date**: 2026-11-14
- **Category**: complexity
- **Authors**: Pramod Padmanabhan, Vladimir Korepin
- **NOFX Relevance Score**: 1

## Abstract

We present a mechanism for obtaining Hilbert-space fragmentation from arbitrary quantum lattice Hamiltonians acting on finite-dimensional local spaces. We first express the Hamiltonian in an algebraic form using supersymmetry (SUSY) and parasupersymmetry (paraSUSY) generators. Enlarging their representations defines a lifting procedure, which we study in two forms, site-wise and bond-wise lifting. Site-wise lifting introduces immobile zero modes that generate exponentially many invariant sectors while retaining the original dynamics in the fully active sector. We determine the sector structure and establish strong fragmentation for uniform lifts at fixed intermediate zero-mode density. For nearest-neighbor chains, correlation functions reveal restricted propagation and, in the mixed-field example, persistent local memory. Bond-wise lifting produces mobile conserved strings, pair-flip dynamics and fluctuating dimers, and extends to fragmented unitary circuits. Different representations and embeddings realize different fragmentation mechanisms. Some of the fragmented models obtained by this method include lifted permutation and Ising spin chains, a lifted toric code, the \(XXC\) families of spin chains, and fragmented extensions derived from the integrable \(H_{1,11}\) and \(H_{1,3}\) seed densities. The last two models are derived from Hietarinta's classification of constant $4\times 4$ Yang-Baxter solutions.
