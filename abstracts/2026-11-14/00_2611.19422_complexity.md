# Hydration-induced local diffusivity heterogeneity modulates aqueous $H_2+OH$ reaction kinetics

- **arXiv**: [2611.19422](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.19422)
- **Date**: 2026-11-14
- **Category**: complexity
- **Authors**: Ming-Gen Li, Shi-Yuan Zhang, Alessio Zaccone
- **NOFX Relevance Score**: 0

## Abstract

At room temperature, a Kramers reaction rate calculated with homogeneous solvent friction exceeds the measured rate of the aqueous reaction $H_2 + OH -> H + H_2O$ by about an order of magnitude. We show that spatially correlated hydration-induced diffusivity fluctuations increase effective friction and suppress the predicted escape rate. Estimates based on hydration-shell properties bring the theoretical rate closer to experiment. The suppression weakens with increasing temperature, linking local solvent heterogeneity to activated aqueous reaction kinetics.
