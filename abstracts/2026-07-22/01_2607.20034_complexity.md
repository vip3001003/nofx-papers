# Motility destabilizes an absorbing-state flock

- **arXiv**: [2607.20034](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20034)
- **Date**: 2026-07-22
- **Category**: complexity
- **Authors**: Narayan Dutt Sharma, Subroto Mukerjee, Chandan Dasgupta et al.
- **NOFX Relevance Score**: 1

## Abstract

Activity, when it takes the form of motility, is generally seen to promote order in many-body systems. Here we present a one-dimensional lattice model that, in the non-motile limit, exhibits absorbing ferromagnetic states. When activity is introduced through biased motility, these absorbing state are destabilised and the system instead undergoes a transition from an ordered flock to a disordered state as the alignment strength is decreased. A finite-size scaling analysis of physical quantities reveals a continuous transition with critical exponents satisfying the hyperscaling relation in one dimension, providing quantitative evidence for the activity-induced disorder.
