# Robust q-negative Multifractal Detrended Cross-Correlation Coefficient

- **arXiv**: [2607.06324](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.06324)
- **Date**: 2026-07-07
- **Category**: complexity
- **Authors**: Thiago B. Murari, José Fernando F. Mendes, Hernane B. B. Pereira et al.
- **NOFX Relevance Score**: 3

## Abstract

The multifractal detrended cross-correlation coefficient $ρ_q(n)$ is widely used to investigate scale-dependent interactions, but its application to negative fluctuation orders is affected by numerical instabilities, unbounded values, and interpretational difficulties. We propose a Signed Multifractal Detrended Cross-Correlation Coefficient, $ρ_{\mathrm{SMFDCCA}}(n,q)$, an amplitude-conditioned correlation observable for multifractal detrended analysis, based on locally normalized detrended correlations and regularized fluctuation amplitudes. The proposed coefficient preserves the sign of local interactions, remains strictly bounded within $[-1,1]$ for both positive and negative values of $q$, and eliminates the corrective procedures required by previous approaches. Validation using independent fractional Gaussian noise confirms the absence of spurious cross-correlations and the numerical stability of the method. Applications demonstrate that the proposed observable resolves how cross-correlations evolve jointly with temporal scale and fluctuation amplitude, revealing scale- and amplitude-dependent correlation structures, including stronger synchronization during large fluctuations in stock-market indices and heterogeneous coupling patterns in temperature records.
