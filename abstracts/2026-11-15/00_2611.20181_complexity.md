# Calculating correlation functions in integrable spin chains from local conserved charges and currents

- **arXiv**: [2611.20181](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.20181)
- **Date**: 2026-11-15
- **Category**: complexity
- **Authors**: Kohei Fukai
- **NOFX Relevance Score**: 0

## Abstract

We develop a simple algebraic method for calculating correlation functions in integrable spin chains. We introduce current minors, antisymmetrized products of local conserved charge densities and their currents, and prove that their expectation values are determinants of density and current mean values. Decomposing local operators into current minors, we derive factorized formulas for correlation functions in terms of current mean values, valid in any nondegenerate eigenstate. Applying the method to the spin-$1/2$ XXZ chain and, more generally, to every representation of the Temperley--Lieb algebra, we prove previously conjectured factorized formulas for short-distance correlation functions.
