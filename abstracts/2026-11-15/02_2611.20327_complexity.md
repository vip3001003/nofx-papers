# Exceptional structure and Lifshitz criticality in a non-Hermitian Kitaev chain

- **arXiv**: [2611.20327](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.20327)
- **Date**: 2026-11-15
- **Category**: complexity
- **Authors**: Aritra Ghosh, Akash Sinha, Miloslav Znojil
- **NOFX Relevance Score**: 2

## Abstract

We study an exactly solvable non-Hermitian $N$-site Kitaev chain with unequal pair-creation and pair-annihilation amplitudes and vanishing intersite hopping. We show that the chain supports an exceptional point (EP) at which all the many-body energies collapse to zero and the largest Jordan block grows quadratically with $N$. By constructing an explicit positive metric for the associated $N$-dimensional one-body problem which admits an extension to the full many-body Hamiltonian, we demonstrate that this EP collapse can be approached through a continuous quasi-Hermitian path in the parameter space. Further, in a different regime with onsite energies alternating between two unequal positive values, we show that pairing amplitudes of opposite signs can lead to crossings between ground states of opposite fermion parity within a quasi-Hermitian regime. Their accumulation in the thermodynamic limit produces a Lifshitz transition, characterized by particle-/hole-pocket formation, quadratic critical dispersion, and a nonanalytic ground-state energy density. We show that this transition remains separated from the EP boundary and ceases to exist for balanced real pairing.
