# Fluctuating chemically active droplets

- **arXiv**: [2610.08154](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08154)
- **Date**: 2026-10-06
- **Category**: complexity
- **Authors**: Guido L. A. Kusters, Noah Ziethen, Michael E. Cates et al.
- **NOFX Relevance Score**: 2

## Abstract

We study size fluctuations of chemically active droplets in the regime of weak reactions and strong phase separation. By combining a thin-interface model for the deterministic droplet growth with stochastic noise based on fluctuation-dissipation arguments, we report excellent quantitative agreement between our theory and numerical field simulations. Finally, we use our model to infer the effective non-equilibrium potential that governs droplet growth, and show that active reactions that enable size control suppress nucleation.
