# Anizotropic Ising Model on 2D Kagomé Lattice as an Inhomogeneous XYZ Integrable Model

- **arXiv**: [2609.10163](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10163)
- **Date**: 2026-09-09
- **Category**: complexity
- **Authors**: Shahane A. Khachatryan, Hrachya Babujian, Ara G. Sedrakyan
- **NOFX Relevance Score**: 0

## Abstract

We investigate a generalized inhomogeneous two-dimensional Ising model on the kagomé lattice with two alternating couplings $J_i$ and $J'_i$, $i=1,2,3$, along the three lattice directions. The local Boltzmann weights are mapped onto a non-symmetric eight-vertex $R$-matrix satisfying the free-fermion condition for arbitrary values of the six couplings. We analyze the Yang--Baxter structure for the cases $J'_i=J_i$ and $J'_i=-J_i$ and derive the corresponding one-dimensional quantum spin chains in the anisotropic limit. The first case yields a transverse-field Ising-type Hamiltonian, while the second leads to a modified chain with a graded permutation structure and shifted partition-function zeros. For a partially anisotropic model, we also obtain the free energy and specific heat.
