# Reconstructing massive quivers on a defect circle

- **arXiv**: [2611.34129](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34129)
- **Date**: 2026-11-25
- **Category**: complexity
- **Authors**: Ting-Kai Chen, Jin Chen
- **NOFX Relevance Score**: 0

## Abstract

We study the kink and particle spectra for the massive $φ_{2,1}$-deformations of minimal models $\mathcal M_{k+1,k+2}$ via truncated conformal space approach (TCSA) on a defect circle. The massive $φ_{2,1}$-deformation preserves a set of non-invertible symmetries, furnishing a fusion category $\mathcal C_k\simeq {\rm SO}(3)_k$. These non-invertible symmetries are spontaneously broken, and thus label the vacua and organize the kinks and particles into their multiplets in terms of quiver diagrams. We employ a finite-volume truncated Hamiltonian approach on defect Hilbert spaces, $\mathcal H_{X_i}$ for $X_i\in\mathcal C_k$, to study these quivers in the IR theories. The symmetry defect can then close an individual kink on a circle. We first establish the relation between states of definite fractional momentum on the defect circle and physical kink and particle states labelled by their vacuum pairs. Using form factors of local fields, we independently measure the squared amplitudes between the states of the two bases in defect TCSA. Combining these measurements with defect TCSA spectra, we reconstruct the quiver diagrams for $k=3,4,5$ without assuming them in advance. In particular, for $k=4$ we reconstruct the distinct quivers of the ordered and disordered phases. For $k=5$, we identify the quivers of both the light and heavy multiplets.
