# Monotonic probes of asymmetry and robustness of the quantum Mpemba effect

- **arXiv**: [2611.34853](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34853)
- **Date**: 2026-11-25
- **Category**: complexity
- **Authors**: Riccardo Travaglino, Michele Mazzoni, Pasquale Calabrese
- **NOFX Relevance Score**: 2

## Abstract

Entanglement asymmetry has emerged as a powerful probe of symmetry restoration and the quantum Mpemba effect. However, its conventional Rényi generalizations, defined as differences of Rényi entropies, are not monotones in the resource-theoretic sense. Here, we investigate an alternative family of asymmetry quantifiers based on sandwiched Rényi divergences, which satisfy monotonicity under symmetry-preserving quantum channels. Developing a two-parameter replica approach, we show how these quantities can be computed from charged moments and establish a remarkable correspondence with conventional Rényi asymmetries under the inversion of the Rényi index, $α\leftrightarrow 1/α$. This relation, exact for pure states, holds at leading order in the relevant scaling limits for homogeneous quantum quenches, including interacting integrable systems, and for Haar-random states. Applying these results to quenches from tilted ferromagnetic states, we demonstrate that the quantum Mpemba effect is remarkably robust, occurring throughout the entire family of sandwiched Rényi asymmetry monotones. We identify dynamical regimes in quenches from spatially inhomogeneous initial states where conventional Rényi asymmetries and genuine monotones exhibit qualitatively different behaviours. We also investigate a solvable toy model illustrating the possible dependence of Mpemba crossings on the Rényi index. Our findings establish a connection between conventional Rényi asymmetries and quantum resource theory, explaining their effectiveness in broad classes of nonequilibrium protocols while highlighting the additional information provided by genuine asymmetry monotones.
