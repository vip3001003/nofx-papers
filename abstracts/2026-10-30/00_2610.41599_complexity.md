# Underdamped Stochastic Optimal Transport with Landauer boundary conditions: machine learning and analytical solution

- **arXiv**: [2610.41599](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41599)
- **Date**: 2026-10-30
- **Category**: complexity
- **Authors**: Julia Sanders, Dario Lucente, Marco Baldovin et al.
- **NOFX Relevance Score**: 0

## Abstract

Finding optimal transport protocols for underdamped stochastic processes is a difficult yet important problem in control theory. In this paper we develop a direct method, based on machine learning, to find the optimal solution of the problem in the presence of non-linear boundary conditions, including those modelling the Landauer bit-erasure problem. The dynamics is steered from a single-peak to a double-peak distribution in position space, by only acting on a controllable external potential. The problem is highly non-linear, and analytical solutions are not available in general. We benchmark our numerical results with a perturbative expansion, which is expected to hold in a suitable overdamped limit.
