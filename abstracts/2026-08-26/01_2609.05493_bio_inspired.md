# Optimizing Train Driving to Minimize the Electricity Cost of an Entire Railway Traffic Mesh using Evolutionary Algorithms

- **arXiv**: [2609.05493](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05493)
- **Date**: 2026-08-26
- **Category**: bio_inspired
- **Authors**: Eduardo Pilo de la Fuente, Sudip K. Mazumder, Maria Antonia Simon
- **NOFX Relevance Score**: 1

## Abstract

This paper presents a procedure to optimize the way trains are driven, which pursues, in addition to fulfilling operational constraints such as admissible speeds or journey durations, the minimization of the cost related to supplying electrical energy to the trains, including the cost of the energy consumption and the cost of the power capacity utilization. This procedure combines: (i) a traffic model that merges the energy and power footprint of each rail service part of the traffic mesh, and (ii) an evolutionary computation framework that enables searching for the optimal way to drive the trains to achieve an optimal traffic mesh. This procedure is applied to a 450 km long section of the high-speed line from Madrid to Barcelona (Spain).
