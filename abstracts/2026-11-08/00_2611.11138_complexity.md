# Odd Darcy Law: Odd Viscosity and Edge Currents in Porous Media

- **arXiv**: [2611.11138](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.11138)
- **Date**: 2026-11-08
- **Category**: complexity
- **Authors**: Raphael Maire, Ignacio Pagonabarraga
- **NOFX Relevance Score**: 0

## Abstract

We derive and numerically validate an odd Darcy law for chiral active fluids in porous media, in which an antisymmetric permeability tensor induces a flow transverse to the applied pressure gradient. We identify two distinct mechanisms leading to this odd response: odd viscosity in the fluid or the nonlinear coupling of edge currents to inertia. We then show how the transverse transport depends on porosity, boundary friction, and chirality.
