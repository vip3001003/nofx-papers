# Ergotropy as distance from equilibrium

- **arXiv**: [2611.11343](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.11343)
- **Date**: 2026-11-08
- **Category**: complexity
- **Authors**: Michele Campisi
- **NOFX Relevance Score**: 1

## Abstract

We show that the ergotropy (a.k.a available energy) of a thermally isolated system is the $1$-Wasserstein distance between two energy probability density functions. Specifically, between the energy profile of the system state, and the energy profile of its associated passive state. We prove this for both classical and quantum systems, and illustrate it with examples. Our result formalises the intuition that ergotropy is a quantifier of distance from equilibrium
