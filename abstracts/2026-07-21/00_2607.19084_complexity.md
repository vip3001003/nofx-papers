# Quasi-stationary and quasi-ergodic distributions in the Pelikan random map

- **arXiv**: [2607.19084](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.19084)
- **Date**: 2026-07-21
- **Category**: complexity
- **Authors**: Samuel Brevitt, Rainer Klages
- **NOFX Relevance Score**: 0

## Abstract

In this paper we present a concrete example of a substochastic discrete-time Markov chain on a countable state space producing a spectrum of infinitely many quasi-stationary distributions (QSDs) for generic parameter values, with each QSD supporting a distinct escape rate. Our system is motivated by an open variant of the Pelikan dynamical system, a random map introduced in the 1980s. These QSDs, and their stability to perturbative random noise, are tested in numerical simulations. The existence of unique QEDs is also established for some parameter values.
