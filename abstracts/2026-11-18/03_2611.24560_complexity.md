# A Monte Carlo study of closely packed two-patch particles with frustrated interactions

- **arXiv**: [2611.24560](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24560)
- **Date**: 2026-11-18
- **Category**: complexity
- **Authors**: Rui Ding, Yan-Wei Li, Hao Hu
- **NOFX Relevance Score**: 3

## Abstract

Recently it was found that closely packed two-patch particles exhibit rich orientational ordering behaviors, e.g., nonmonotonic changes of the nematic order parameter and double BKT phase transitions [arXiv:2509.03981]. In this work, we show that, with repulsive patch-patch, attractive patch-nonpatch and intermediate nonpatch-nonpatch interactions, there exist frustrations in the system of closely packed two-patch particles. To investigate the ground states of the system, we design a Markov Chain Monte Carlo algorithm with single-defect propagation. The simulation results show that the ground states are disordered and the correlation functions decay exponentially with the distance. These results demonstrate that in 2D close packing one could design patchy particles to explore orientationally disordered systems.
