# Geometric Decomposition of Entropy Production Based on General $f$-Divergences

- **arXiv**: [2611.24024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24024)
- **Date**: 2026-11-18
- **Category**: complexity
- **Authors**: Rihito Nagase, Shoki Sugimoto, Takahiro Sagawa
- **NOFX Relevance Score**: 0

## Abstract

We construct a general class of excess--housekeeping decompositions of entropy production based on $f$-divergences for overdamped Langevin systems. The excess entropy production rate equals the relaxation rate of the $f$-divergence toward a relaxation target and also admits an optimal-transport representation, while the housekeeping velocity preserves the same relaxation target. Our framework recovers the Hatano--Sasa and Maes--Neto{č}n{ý} decompositions as special cases, and unifies their relaxational and optimal-transport aspects within a common structure. Moreover, our framework yields physically meaningful decompositions beyond these two by taking a desired distribution as the relaxation target.
