# Phase transitions in work fluctuations in correlated N-particle resets

- **arXiv**: [2611.24318](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24318)
- **Date**: 2026-11-18
- **Category**: complexity
- **Authors**: Felix Herber, Deepak Gupta, Supriya Krishnamurthy
- **NOFX Relevance Score**: 1

## Abstract

Systems of $N$ independent uncorrelated particles subjected to the same stochastic change in the environment have recently been studied both theoretically and in experiments, to elucidate the interesting phenomenon of dynamically emergent interparticle positional correlations. However, the thermodynamic cost of creating such correlations, as well as its interplay with the initial distribution of the $N$ particles has not been studied. In this paper, we study systems of $N$ particles that are simultaneously subjected to a switch of an external potential, either stochastically or periodically, beginning from arbitrarily correlated initial states. We map out the distribution of thermodynamic work for such a system in the full parameter space of the problem that includes the number of particles $N$, arbitrary initial correlations between particles and arbitrary initial reduced single particle distributions. We demonstrate that the parameter space is partitioned non-trivially, and in an $N$-dependent way, into distinct "phases", whereby the functional form of the variance of the work changes discontinuously across phases. The number of such phases depends on the source of stochasticity in the dynamics.
