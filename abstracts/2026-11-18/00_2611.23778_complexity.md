# Cumulant Generating Function of the Hill-Lane-Sudderth urn model

- **arXiv**: [2611.23778](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23778)
- **Date**: 2026-11-18
- **Category**: complexity
- **Authors**: Simone Franchini
- **NOFX Relevance Score**: 0

## Abstract

We consider the Hill, Lane and Sudderth urn, in which a black or white ball is added at each step with probabilities depending on the current proportion of black balls. We solve the equation for the limiting normalized cumulant generating function when the urn function is invertible on the outer branches beyond the fixed points and the corresponding inverses are Lipschitz. The solution has a parametric representation through three quadratures of the urn function and a limit of compositions of radicals, with no derivatives or inverses of the urn function in the final formula. We prove an error bound that does not accumulate along the partition and remains valid when starting from the initial singularity. Four examples with exact solutions illustrate the two orientations of the construction, the non-affine case and the presence of zeros.
