# Localized active phase in the coevolving voter model with fixed links

- **arXiv**: [2611.23870](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23870)
- **Date**: 2026-11-18
- **Category**: complexity
- **Authors**: Hoseung Jang, Maxi San Miguel, Byungjoon Min
- **NOFX Relevance Score**: 0

## Abstract

The coevolving voter model (CVM) describes the evolution of node states and network topology through copying and rewiring dynamics. It traditionally assumes that all ties can be rewired, overlooking unbreakable real-world relationships like family or close friendships. To address this, we introduce fixed links that cannot be rewired and study how these structural constraints modify the collective dynamics of the CVM. In addition to the active and frozen phases of the CVM, we find a new localized active phase in which activity is sustained, beyond a percolation transition, by a giant component of fixed links. The existence of this new phase is predicted by a moment-closure approximation that goes beyond the standard pair approximation. The finite-size manifestation of this phase is an asymmetric fragmented phase. In addition, the active-frozen transition of the CVM, manifested as a fragmentation transition for finite systems, is delayed by the existence of fixed links to larger values of the rewiring rate.
