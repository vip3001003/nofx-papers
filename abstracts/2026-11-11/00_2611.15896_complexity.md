# U(1)-Symmetric Spin SYK Models: Ordering, Chaos, and Transport

- **arXiv**: [2611.15896](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15896)
- **Date**: 2026-11-11
- **Category**: complexity
- **Authors**: Shiyong Guo, Enrico Rinaldi, Jad C. Halimeh et al.
- **NOFX Relevance Score**: 0

## Abstract

The Sachdev--Ye--Kitaev (SYK) model provides an analytically controlled setting for strongly interacting quantum dynamics and holography, but its fermionic formulation leads to nonlocal encoding overheads on qubit-based quantum processors. We study a U(1)-symmetric spin analogue of the complex SYK model, constructed from random charge-conserving $q$-spin interactions that are native to a qubit Hilbert space. In the large-$N$, large-$q$ limit, the model reproduces the leading saddle-point structure of complex fermionic SYK under the identification of chemical potential with magnetic field and charge density with magnetization. We investigate the finite-$N$, finite-$q$ regime using exact diagonalization. A U(1)-invariant ordering susceptibility increases with system size for $q = 2, 4$ but decreases for $q = 6$, while bulk-spectrum level statistics are consistent with the Gaussian orthogonal ensemble at half filling and the Gaussian unitary ensemble in the adjacent magnetization sector. We further construct coupled-cluster chains and obtain domain-wall dynamics consistent with diffusive magnetization transport in a small-cluster regime accessible to matrix-product-state simulations. These results establish analytical benchmarks and finite-size signatures of ordering, quantum chaos, and magnetization transport in U(1)-symmetric spin-SYK models, and they motivate quantum simulation of the low-temperature, large-system regime, which remains difficult for classical methods.
