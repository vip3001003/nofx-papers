# REMBGO for Evaluation-Limited Optimization: CEC2022 Benchmarking and EAF-STATCOM Generalization

- **arXiv**: [2610.13471](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13471)
- **Date**: 2026-10-09
- **Category**: bio_inspired
- **Authors**: Vikram Chekuri, Narapareddy Ramarao, Prashanth N A
- **NOFX Relevance Score**: 0

## Abstract

Metaheuristic comparisons are often reported at evaluation budgets far larger than an expensive engineering simulation can afford. This paper presents REMBGO, an evaluation-budget-aware refinement of EMBGO. Its contribution is not any single operator: it is the integration of population preservation, scale-normalized exploration and recovery mechanisms whose parent-relative benefit is concentrated under restricted function-evaluation budgets. REMBGO is evaluated on CEC2022 at a primary, application-aligned budget of 1040 function evaluations, with a surrounding budget ladder reported descriptively to characterise budget dependence rather than as a family of controlled hypotheses. A component-removal study finds no individual mechanism whose removal survives multiplicity correction, consistent with an integrated rather than a single-operator contribution. The algorithm is then applied to EAF--STATCOM controller tuning, and the resulting frozen gain vectors are transferred without retuning to twenty pre-specified unseen stochastic arc realizations ($210$ fixed-gain simulations, all feasible). The selected gain-vector main effect accounts for only $3.6$--$5.8\%$ of the crossed validation variation, against approximately $61.4\%$ for interaction plus residual, and the matched REMBGO--EMBGO paired differences are controller-dependent in sign and not statistically significant. REMBGO is therefore presented as a resource-aware refinement whose benefit must be stated together with the evaluation budget, not as a universally superior optimizer.
