# Suppression of gel formation by spontaneous fragmentation

- **arXiv**: [2611.09537](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09537)
- **Date**: 2026-11-06
- **Category**: complexity
- **Authors**: Sergey Matveev
- **NOFX Relevance Score**: 0

## Abstract

In this work we study the discrete Smoluchowski equation with a multiplicative kernel and an additional term describing the spontaneous shattering of each particle into monomers at a constant rate $λ$. We show that the introduction of fragmentation suppresses gelation for $λ\ge 4$. We derive analytical expressions for the second moment and the monomer concentration $n_1(t)$. These expressions lead to a recurrence relation allowing the steady-state particle size distribution for $λ\ge 4$ to be calculated with machine precision. We validate our analytical and semi-analytical results via numerical simulations using a solver that employs the Fast Fourier Transform for computation of the right-hand side and a second-order Runge-Kutta method with adaptive time steps.
