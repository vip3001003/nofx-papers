# Effective particle size governs structure and dynamics in rough hard-particle fluids

- **arXiv**: [2609.08699](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.08699)
- **Date**: 2026-09-08
- **Category**: complexity
- **Authors**: Nanqing Xiao, Zhen Zhang, Walter Kob et al.
- **NOFX Relevance Score**: 0

## Abstract

We numerically investigate how particle surface roughness affects the static and dynamic properties of a hard-particle fluid across a wide range of densities, rho. These simulations of a simple model of granular systems reveal that, although the amplitude and coverage of surface corrugation significantly influence the rho-dependence of system properties, the qualitative behavior of this dependence remains unchanged. These findings can be described quantitatively by introducing an effective particle size, which enables a direct mapping of the rough particle systems to equivalent hard-disk systems. Analytical calculations provide an explicit form of this mapping, and allow us to predict the static and dynamic properties of rough particles using standard liquid-state theories for hard disks.
