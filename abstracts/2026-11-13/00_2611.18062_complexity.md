# Exact stochastic theory of post-transcriptional gene autoregulation mediated by RNA-binding proteins

- **arXiv**: [2611.18062](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18062)
- **Date**: 2026-11-13
- **Category**: complexity
- **Authors**: Chen Jia
- **NOFX Relevance Score**: 0

## Abstract

While analytical theories of transcriptional feedback loops have been well established, exact stochastic analyses of post-transcriptional feedback loops remain relatively scarce and are notoriously challenging. Here, we develop an exact theory of post-transcriptional gene autoregulation, in which reversible protein-mRNA binding modulates both translation and mRNA stability. We derive the state-dependent protein burst-size distribution, the steady-state protein number distribution in terms of confluent Heun functions, and an exact criterion for sub-Poissonian fluctuations. By comparing transcriptional and post-transcriptional networks, we reveal a reversal in their relative advantages in noise suppression and rhythm transmission: negative feedback favors post-transcriptional regulation, whereas positive feedback favors transcriptional regulation. Our results reveal how the regulatory pattern fundamentally shapes gene expression fluctuations and dynamic responses.
