# Magnetic tuning of odd elastic instabilities in chiral active crystals

- **arXiv**: [2611.21876](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21876)
- **Date**: 2026-11-16
- **Category**: complexity
- **Authors**: Umberto Marini Bettolo Marconi, Lorenzo Caprini
- **NOFX Relevance Score**: 2

## Abstract

We investigate the dynamical properties of an odd active crystal whose elementary units follow an inertial dynamics and experience a Lorentz force induced by an external magnetic field. The continuous injection of energy associated with active chirality gives rise to odd elasticity, manifested through transverse forces between the crystal constituents. We show that the interplay between the magnetic field and these transverse forces produces a distinctive long-wavelength instability at vanishing wavevector, which is absent in both non-magnetic elastic crystals and passive crystals subjected to a magnetic field. Moreover, the magnetic field provides a means of tuning the collective oscillations of the crystal, enabling the emergence of exceptional points and tuning the dynamical instability induced by odd elasticity at finite wavevectors. Our analytical results yield explicit predictions for the displacement spectrum, static structure factor, and intermediate scattering function. These findings suggest that magnetic fields could provide a powerful tool for controlling collective phenomena in high-density systems of granular or colloidal spinners.
