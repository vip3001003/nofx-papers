# Emptiness formation in quantum fluids: a hydrodynamic solution

- **arXiv**: [2611.21581](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21581)
- **Date**: 2026-11-16
- **Category**: complexity
- **Authors**: Alexander G. Abanov, Boris A. Khanikati
- **NOFX Relevance Score**: 0

## Abstract

We develop an auxiliary-kernel method for constructing the hydrodynamic instantons that govern macroscopic emptiness formation in one-dimensional Galilean quantum fluids. The key step is to represent the equation of state by an auxiliary integral kernel inspired by the Bethe ansatz. For equations of state admitting such a representation, we reduce the imaginary-time hydrodynamic problem to a single integral equation. Its solution determines the full density and current profiles, the vacuum boundary, and the leading emptiness rate. Notably, the construction does not require microscopic integrability. For the Lieb--Liniger gas, we derive the previously conjectured integral equation for the leading rate and use a known elliptic solution to obtain explicit expressions for the rate and vacuum boundary. Direct hydrodynamic numerics support the predicted boundary. We also recover known instanton solutions for a family of polytropic fluids.
