# A simple solvable $Z_N$ Hamiltonian in any dimension

- **arXiv**: [2611.20874](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.20874)
- **Date**: 2026-11-16
- **Category**: complexity
- **Authors**: Yuguan Li, Murray T. Batchelor
- **NOFX Relevance Score**: 0

## Abstract

We construct local $Z_N$ clock Hamiltonians from Baxter-Fendley chains coupled according to an arbitrary finite oriented simple transverse graph. Bounded edge lengths and vertex degrees give finite-range multispin interactions, with $2d$-site bulk terms on a $d$-dimensional rectangular lattice. For open longitudinal boundaries, an explicit nonlocal unitary transformation decouples the chains and gives a free parafermion spectrum. On rectangular lattices this holds for any combination of open and periodic transverse boundaries. For periodic longitudinal boundaries, neighbouring chain charges fix the closing-bond twists. In each joint charge sector, the energies are sums of the corresponding twisted periodic-chain energies. As an example, we construct a three-dimensional array of chains perpendicular to a Penrose rhombus tiling.
