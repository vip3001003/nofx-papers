# Dynamical theory of the East model. I. A review on the Zwanzig-Mori projection-operator formalism

- **arXiv**: [2611.20946](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.20946)
- **Date**: 2026-11-16
- **Category**: complexity
- **Authors**: Chong Zha, Yuliang Jin
- **NOFX Relevance Score**: 0

## Abstract

The East model is a paradigmatic kinetically constrained spin model in one dimension that exhibits glassy dynamics. This review surveys the progress on the theoretical description of the dynamics of the East model, focusing on the spin time correlation function and the relaxation time. We summarize rigorous results on the super-Arrhenius scaling of the relaxation time in the low-temperature limit. We then present a unified derivation of the Zwanzig-Mori projection-operator formalism for both the fluctuation and domain bases. Within this formalism, an infinite hierarchy of coupled equations for the time correlation function and memory kernels emerges---a structure reminiscent of generalized mode-coupling theory developed for supercooled liquids. We review the analytical approximations obtained by truncating or closing the hierarchy at first or second orders and compare them against Monte Carlo simulations. Although these approaches perform reasonably at moderate temperatures, they universally fail in the low-temperature regime: mode-coupling closure schemes in the fluctuation basis introduce spurious ergodicity-breaking transitions, while truncations in the domain basis discard slower modes and fail to capture the asymptotic divergence of the relaxation time. The loss of analytical tractability beyond second order remains the central obstacle, underscoring the fundamental difficulty of describing hierarchical relaxation even in this ``simplest'' model of glassy dynamics.
