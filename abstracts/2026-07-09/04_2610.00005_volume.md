# A Multi-Venue Solana/DeFi Microstructure Data Corpus: The RED-2400 Family v2

- **arXiv**: [2610.00005](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00005)
- **Date**: 2026-07-09
- **Category**: volume
- **Authors**: Arati Uday Kamat
- **NOFX Relevance Score**: 4

## Abstract

Empirical research on decentralized and Solana-native market microstructure is constrained less by method than by data: the strongest results in the field rest on a small number of Ethereum-centric datasets, and public, record-level tapes of Solana venues, cross-chain flows, and on-chain oracle behavior remain scarce. We release and document the RED-2400 family v2, a corpus of five public benchmark datasets assembled by a single public-blockchain observer over a concurrent 57-day 2026 window (2026-05-08 to 2026-07-05) under one methodology (free-tier and public feeds only, no paid data, no private accounts, no trading), each with fixed-seed reproducibility scripts, SHA-256 manifests, and a CC-BY-4.0 licence. The five datasets characterize, respectively, Pyth oracle staleness against centralized-exchange prices (164,002 observations), an Aave utilization and Ethereum liquidation panel (18,750 liquidations plus a four-chain utilization series), spot-perpetual basis and funding coupling (244,719 observations), a Solana CEX-versus-DEX spread tape with realized impact (328,186 observations), and a Wormhole cross-chain source-flow tape (360,714 messages). Across the corpus we report three descriptive findings, one honest null, and one field-completeness characterization; none is presented as a causal estimate. The contribution is a coherent, reproducible benchmark family that complements the Ethereum canon and lowers the data barrier for Solana and cross-chain microstructure research.
