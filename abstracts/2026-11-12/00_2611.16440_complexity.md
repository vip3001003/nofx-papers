# Thermal Hall sign reversal from competing magnons and magnon pairs in pyrochlore spin-nematic magnets

- **arXiv**: [2611.16440](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16440)
- **Date**: 2026-11-12
- **Category**: complexity
- **Authors**: Tetsuya Sato, Emika Takata, Masahiro Sato
- **NOFX Relevance Score**: 0

## Abstract

Bound magnon pairs are central to spin-nematic order, yet experimentally distinguishing them from single magnons remains challenging. We show that their Berry-curvature-driven heat transport provides a characteristic signature in the thermal Hall response. For a spin-$3/2$ pyrochlore antiferromagnet with ferromagnetic biquadratic exchange in the magnetic-field-induced fully polarized phase, we construct single-magnon bands together with magnon-pair bands, include the Dzyaloshinskii--Moriya (DM) interaction, and evaluate their intrinsic thermal Hall conductivities. The lowest magnon and magnon-pair bands carry Berry curvatures of opposite sign. Because the gaps of the two quasiparticles respond differently to magnetic field, their competing contributions can drive both magnetic-field- and temperature-induced sign reversals of the thermal Hall conductivity. Estimates for chromium spinel oxides indicate that the effect can approach experimentally accessible magnitudes. We also find that the hybridization between the one- and two-magnon sectors induced by the DM interaction preserves this qualitative signature. These results suggest that a broad class of frustrated magnets with both magnons and magnon pairs provides a possible route to tuning the sign of the thermal Hall conductivity and probing bound magnon pairs through thermal transport experiments.
