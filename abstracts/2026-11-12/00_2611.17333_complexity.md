# Discrete Time Crystals from Anti-Unitary Floquet Driving and Dynamical Freezing

- **arXiv**: [2611.17333](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17333)
- **Date**: 2026-11-12
- **Category**: complexity
- **Authors**: Anirban Das, Asmi Haldar, Arnab Das
- **NOFX Relevance Score**: 0

## Abstract

We show anti-unitary periodic drive paves a direct path for stabilizing a Discrete Time-Crystal by Dynamical Freezing (DF). The magnetization appears as an emergent conserved operator, and an exact pairing of two-cycle quasi-energies pins the subharmonic response. DF keeps the effective Hamiltonian $\textit{quasi-local}$; infinite-chain simulations show no detectable decay for a generic state with any finite magnetization. We outline an experimental realization of the anti-unitary drive.
