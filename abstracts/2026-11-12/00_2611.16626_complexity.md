# Quantum tunneling of a measurement-driven active particle

- **arXiv**: [2611.16626](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16626)
- **Date**: 2026-11-12
- **Category**: complexity
- **Authors**: Tobias Nadolny, Matteo Brunelli, Christoph Bruder et al.
- **NOFX Relevance Score**: 0

## Abstract

Active particles can exhibit persistent motion set by their orientation. We demonstrate this key property for a quantum active particle: The particle's motion is locked to the orientation of its spin due to spin-orbit coupling and damping, and the orientation is made persistent through continuous measurement via the quantum Zeno effect. As a result, individual quantum trajectories feature an active run-and-tumble motion. When the particle impinges on a potential barrier, its persistent velocity leads to accumulation near the barrier, in contrast with the reflection of a free quantum particle. We show that quantum tunneling enhances the active particle's transmission rate compared to that of an analogous classical particle.
