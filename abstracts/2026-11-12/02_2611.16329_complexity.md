# Information Thermodynamics in Closed Quantum Many-Body Dynamics

- **arXiv**: [2611.16329](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16329)
- **Date**: 2026-11-12
- **Category**: complexity
- **Authors**: Yusuke O. Nakai, Shoki Sugimoto, Ryusuke Hamazaki
- **NOFX Relevance Score**: 2

## Abstract

Although information thermodynamics explains how the second law is modified due to the information acquired by Maxwell's demon, its formulation in many-body systems remains largely open. Here, we develop an information-thermodynamic framework for closed quantum many-body systems initially in microscopic thermal equilibrium (MITE), under physically motivated restrictions: local quantum measurements and finite-time controls generated by local Hamiltonians. By additionally assuming that each local feedback term depends only on nearby measurement outcomes, we rigorously prove that the extractable work coincides in the thermodynamic limit with that extractable from the corresponding canonical ensemble. We further express this extractable work in terms of quantum-information quantities, including the quantum-classical mutual information and the Holevo information for the corresponding canonical ensemble. We also show that feedback based on distant measurement outcomes can exploit long-range correlations hidden in MITE, yielding an extensive increase in extracted work relative to the canonical ensemble.
