# Topological insulators on site-eliminated, projected, and patterned fractal lattices

- **arXiv**: [2611.33155](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33155)
- **Date**: 2026-11-24
- **Category**: complexity
- **Authors**: Aiden Mains, Danile Salib, Christopher Leong et al.
- **NOFX Relevance Score**: 0

## Abstract

Identifying fractal lattices of fractional (Hausdorff) dimension as geometric descendants of parent crystals, we showcase the successful realization of canonical topological insulators in one-, two-, and three-dimensional crystals on their self-similar descents using three complementary approaches, starting from lattice-regularized models in integer dimensions. (a) In the first approach, we construct the fractal Hamiltonian by removing all parent-lattice sites that do not belong to such a network (method of site elimination). (b) In the second one, we integrate or project out the same set of sites to obtain an effective Hamiltonian for the fractal lattice (method of projection or renormalization). (c) In the third approach, we retain the parent-crystal lattice intact but spatially pattern the on-site mass parameter, which controls the topology of the parent insulating system, such that it assumes two topologically distinct values on the fractal network and its complement (patterned fractal lattices). By numerically diagonalizing the resulting Hamiltonians and computing appropriate topological invariants or local markers, corroborating the bulk-boundary correspondence through gapless modes at outer or inner boundaries, we establish Su-Schrieffer-Heeger topology on the Cantor set using all three approaches, Haldane topology on the hexaflake [using (a) and (b)] and Qi-Wu-Zhang topology on the Sierpiński carpet [using (c)], and strong $Z_2$ topological insulating phases on the tiled Menger sponge from methods (a), (b), and (c). These findings complement and substantially extend the previously reported realization of Qi-Wu-Zhang topology on the Sierpiński carpet using methods (a) and (b) [Phys. Rev. B 110, L241302 (2024)].
