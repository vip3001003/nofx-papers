# Identifying Renormalization Group Interfaces of Massless WZW Flows $\widehat{\mathfrak{su}}(2)_k \to \widehat{\mathfrak{su}}(2)_1$

- **arXiv**: [2611.33354](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33354)
- **Date**: 2026-11-24
- **Category**: complexity
- **Authors**: Yuma Furuta, Yuya Kusuki, Toshiki Onagi et al.
- **NOFX Relevance Score**: 0

## Abstract

A renormalization-group interface encodes how a flow relates operators at short and long distances. We identify this interface up to topological dressing for the conjectured massless $\widehat{\mathfrak{su}}(2)_k \to \widehat{\mathfrak{su}}(2)_1$ Wess-Zumino-Witten flows at every odd level $k\geq 3$. Global $SU(2)$ symmetry requires the interface to preserve a diagonal current algebra at level $k+1$, built from the currents on its two sides. The $\mathbb{Z}_2$ center symmetry then selects which representation labels the interface. This identification predicts distinct patterns of low-lying interface excitations for flows to the same infrared fixed point. Spin-chain calculations reproduce these patterns for $k=3$ and $5$, providing independent evidence for both conjectured flows.
