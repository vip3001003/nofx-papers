# On fluctuation properties of the cumulative current in gTASEP on open chains

- **arXiv**: [2611.32495](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32495)
- **Date**: 2026-11-24
- **Category**: complexity
- **Authors**: N. C. Pesheva, N. Zh. Bunzarova, E. K. Mitsova et al.
- **NOFX Relevance Score**: 0

## Abstract

We present preliminary results of our numerical study of the fluctuation properties of the cumulative current, JT, in the generalized totally asymmetric simple exclusion process (gTASEP) on open chains with Liggett boundary conditions. GTASEP is the ordinary TASEP (a model of 1D- traffic on a single track, having many diverse applications), where an additional term controlling clustering of the particles is added. Extensive numerical simulations are performed to determine the dependence of the second scaled cumulant C2 of the time integrated current, JT, on the system size, L, in the long-time limit, allowing for comparison between open and periodic geometries. Preliminary results for C2 in the maximum current phase show agreement with theoretical predictions about the Kardar-Parizi-Zhang universality of the gTASEP on a segment.
