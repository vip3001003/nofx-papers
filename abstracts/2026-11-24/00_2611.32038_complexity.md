# Exact Response Theory based on Karhunen-Loève Expansions for Brownian Oscillators

- **arXiv**: [2611.32038](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32038)
- **Date**: 2026-11-24
- **Category**: complexity
- **Authors**: Matteo Colangeli, Lamberto Rondoni, Pasquale Vozza
- **NOFX Relevance Score**: 0

## Abstract

We extend Exact Response Theory (ERT) to an overdamped Brownian oscillator driven by thermal white noise. The stochastic forcing is represented by a Karhunen--Loève series and embedded into an autonomous, augmented phase space using a harmonic auxiliary phase. This representation allows us to construct the ERT dissipation function for fixed mode amplitudes and recover stochastic averages by averaging over the amplitudes and the initial phase. For the variance, we derive the response at finite spectral truncation and show analytically that, with uniform phase sampling, the full-spectrum white-noise limit reproduces the generator-based result for a sudden temperature change. Furthermore, a comparison with first-order linear response theory highlights how this may fail in general. In particular, if the noise-induced variance is quadratic in the noise amplitude, it is completely omitted by linear response. The analysis provides a framework for this model and for studying more general forcing; extensions to non-Markovian dynamics require separate convergence and regularity analyses.
