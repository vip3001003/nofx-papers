# Correlation functions of 2D critical models via a KPZ relation for conformal blocks

- **arXiv**: [2611.32485](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32485)
- **Date**: 2026-11-24
- **Category**: complexity
- **Authors**: Gefei Cai, Shengzhi Jin, Xin Sun et al.
- **NOFX Relevance Score**: 1

## Abstract

The Knizhnik--Polyakov--Zamolodchikov (KPZ) relation allows scaling exponents of two-dimensional (2D) critical systems to be derived from their counterparts in Liouville quantum gravity. We extend this framework to correlation functions based on an integral inversion relation between Virasoro conformal blocks with complementary central charges $c$ and $26-c$. We demonstrate this method in various models by deriving the 2-point disk correlation from their quantum counterpart and the KPZ relation. Results on critical loop models were previously known, but our method is robust enough to include many scenarios previously unsolved, such as the Fuzzy Potts model. Our approach is complementary to the conformal bootstrap.
