# Recovery of Extended Modes from the Non-Hermitian Skin Effect Due to Local Nonlinearity on a Boundary

- **arXiv**: [2611.32783](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32783)
- **Date**: 2026-11-24
- **Category**: complexity
- **Authors**: Kazuki Sone, Tomonari Mizoguchi, Yasuhiro Hatsugai
- **NOFX Relevance Score**: 0

## Abstract

Localization in continuum spectra known as the non-Hermitian skin effect is widely believed to be a consequence of nontrivial topology of bulk bands. However, the robustness of the non-Hermitian skin effect against nonlinear effects is still a nontrivial problem. We here reveal that single-bond nonlinearity on a boundary can recover extended modes in a non-Hermitian tight-binding model. We propose and analyze a one-dimensional minimal model, where we introduce a nonlinear phase shift of a hopping term only at boundary sites. We also propose a possible photonic setup to dynamically observe the nonlinearity-induced extended modes as a steady state. Our results thus challenge the conventional notion of the non-Hermitian skin effect by revealing its dependence on nonlinear boundary conditions.
