# Conformality lost in Anderson localization in infinite dimensions, and regained in finite dimensions: a phenomenological theory of the Anderson transition in high dimensions

- **arXiv**: [2611.32725](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32725)
- **Date**: 2026-11-24
- **Category**: complexity
- **Authors**: Antonello Scardicchio, Carlo Vanoni
- **NOFX Relevance Score**: 0

## Abstract

We show that the recent works on Anderson localization when the number of spatial dimensions $d\to\infty$ can be framed into the scenario dubbed it conformality lost [Kaplan et al., Phys. Rev. D 80, 125005 (2009)], which occurs when two fixed points of the RG collide and become complex. This framing makes it natural for a two-parameter scaling flow à la Berezinskii--Kosterlitz--Thouless (BKT) to control all the observables of the theory as conjectured in [Vanoni et al., PNAS 121, e2401955121 (2024)]. We also use this as a starting point for an $ε$ expansion of the RG equations in $ε=1/d\ll 1$, and find phenomenological RG equations which fit the numerical results in dimensions $d\gtrsim 4$.
