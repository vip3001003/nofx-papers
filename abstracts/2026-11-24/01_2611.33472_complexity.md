# Optimal Controlled Quantum Instrument for Monitored Many-Body Dynamics

- **arXiv**: [2611.33472](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33472)
- **Date**: 2026-11-24
- **Category**: complexity
- **Authors**: Jimin Li
- **NOFX Relevance Score**: 1

## Abstract

Post-selecting rare trajectories of monitored quantum dynamics requires exponentially many repeated experiments. We extend Bayes' rule to monitored many-body dynamics and bias the quantum trajectories by nonlinear functions of the post-measurement state. For functions that are constant on the sets of a suitable finite partition of the post-measurement states, we construct a quantum instrument on the system and an additional register that produces the biased trajectories at the optimal rates without post-selection. This optimal controlled quantum instrument has a natural tensor-network representation as the left canonical form of the system--register tensors. We illustrate the construction with two examples: a precessing qubit, biased by its spin component along the field, and free fermions with gain and loss, biased by the particle number, where the biased trajectories undergo a phase transition. We also argue, under one assumption, that biasing a measured Majorana chain by its von Neumann entropy leads to entanglement transitions.
