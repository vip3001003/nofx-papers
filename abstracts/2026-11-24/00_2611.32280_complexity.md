# Relaxation dynamics of the impurity momentum in a one-dimensional Fermi gas

- **arXiv**: [2611.32280](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32280)
- **Date**: 2026-11-24
- **Category**: complexity
- **Authors**: Felipe Taha Sant'Ana
- **NOFX Relevance Score**: 0

## Abstract

We consider a single mobile impurity coupled by a repulsive contact interaction to a gas of free spinless fermions of equal mass in one dimension. Using the determinant overlaps between the interacting eigenstates and free-fermion states, we derive closed determinant formulas for the matrix elements of the impurity momentum operator and of its square between arbitrary Bethe-ansatz eigenstates of a finite system. At infinite coupling we prove a selection rule: both operators have vanishing matrix elements between distinct eigenstates that share the same rapidity, and every eigenstate carries the per-particle share of the total momentum. For an impurity injected into the Fermi sea we have the following: at infinite coupling the average impurity momentum relaxes completely in the thermodynamic limit, while at finite coupling relaxation remains incomplete. The closed-form matrix elements turn the spectral representation of the average impurity momentum into explicit finite sums over eigenstates. We evaluate the post-injection dynamics, resolve the quantum-flutter oscillations and the approach towards the diagonal-ensemble value, and map the stationary momentum as a function of the injection momentum and the coupling. Finally, we analyse the thermodynamic limit and its asymptotics as an integral over Fredholm determinants.
