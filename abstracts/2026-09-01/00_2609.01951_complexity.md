# Universal scaling and protocol-dependent amplitude in hybrid quantum walks

- **arXiv**: [2609.01951](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01951)
- **Date**: 2026-09-01
- **Category**: complexity
- **Authors**: Cook Hyun Kim, Jaesub Park, Namshik Han
- **NOFX Relevance Score**: 0

## Abstract

Quantum walks spread ballistically but become diffusive with classical admixture. We compare two hybrid walks using identical quantum and classical steps at the same classical-step rate but ordered differently in time. Any nonzero admixture yields diffusion. Near the quantum limit, both diffusion coefficients have first-order poles, but their amplitudes differ by about a factor of two. The coherence timescale sets the pole order, while temporal organization sets the amplitude. We expect our analytical framework to apply broadly to quantum--classical hybrid systems.
