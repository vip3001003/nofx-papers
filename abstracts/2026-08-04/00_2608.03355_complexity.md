# Discrepancy between the H-Function and Entropy: Insights into the rigorously established Boltzmann equation for hard-sphere gases

- **arXiv**: [2608.03355](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03355)
- **Date**: 2026-08-04
- **Category**: complexity
- **Authors**: Li-Xiang Cen
- **NOFX Relevance Score**: 0

## Abstract

By investigating the origin of the evolutionary discrepancy between the H-function and entropy, we elucidate that the H-function fails to serve as a valid arrow-of-time criterion in the rigorously derived Boltzmann equation for the hard-sphere gas model under the Boltzmann-Grad limit.
