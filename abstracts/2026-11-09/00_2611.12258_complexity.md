# On transition probabilities for the ASEP on the half-line

- **arXiv**: [2611.12258](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12258)
- **Date**: 2026-11-09
- **Category**: complexity
- **Authors**: Alexandr Garbali, Cengiz Gazi, Jan de Gier et al.
- **NOFX Relevance Score**: 0

## Abstract

We study the asymmetric simple exclusion process on the half-line with particle insertion and no particle removal at the boundary. We point out that there exist two distinct multiple contour integral formulas for the transition probability between two finite particle configurations containing the same number of particles. We recall one of these formulas from the literature and prove a second, new formula by showing that it satisfies the evolution equation using a coordinate Bethe ansatz calculation and verify that the initial condition is implemented by the contour integrals. As an application we sum this formula over all final configurations and obtain an integral formula for the probability that the number of particles is conserved up to a given time. The proofs use symmetric rational functions and their properties together with contour deformations.
