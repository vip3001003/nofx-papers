# Holonomy, Spectral Topology, and Detailed Balance in Classical and Open Quantum Systems

- **arXiv**: [2611.12514](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12514)
- **Date**: 2026-11-09
- **Category**: complexity
- **Authors**: Alvan Hunt
- **NOFX Relevance Score**: 3

## Abstract

Band topology and non-Hermitian spectral theory are now applied to mechanical metamaterials, chiral active fluids, stochastic networks, and open quantum systems. We give a tutorial synthesis organized by holonomy and by one question: what can the spectral data of a linear generator reveal about detailed balance? Three holonomies recur: the Berry phase of a band, the Burgers vector of a dislocation (the integrated torsion of the defect geometry), and the cycle affinity of a Markov network (the holonomy of the connection $\ln(W_{ij}/W_{ji})$). We rederive the Chern numbers $C_m=m(\mathrm{sgn}\,Ω+\mathrm{sgn}\,ν_o)$ of a compressible odd-viscous fluid and restate the dislocation zero-mode count of Paulose, Chen, and Vitelli as a pairing of integrated torsion with topological polarization. Our central message is that spectral topology can obstruct detailed balance but does not determine the magnitude of dissipation. Detailed balance is self-adjointness in the inner product fixed by the stationary state, so a non-real eigenvalue, an exceptional point (EP), or a nonzero point-gap winding number of a Markov generator rules it out and forces entropy production $σ>0$, with $σ\ge k_B j^2/D$ for any current, whereas a real, diagonalizable spectrum does not in general certify it. In the Hatano-Nelson ring, which is normal yet has complex spectrum, $σ$ sweeps $(0,\infty)$ at fixed winding number. EPs need a separate assessment of the dynamics: with generalized detailed balance including time-reversal parity, an EP is compatible with equilibrium only through time-reversal-odd dynamics, as at critical damping of a Langevin oscillator, whereas an EP of a system with only even variables, such as an odd elastic medium, certifies a nonequilibrium steady state; in both cases $σ$ is analytic across the EP. We propose benchtop tests for each statement.
