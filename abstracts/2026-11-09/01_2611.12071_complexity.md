# Matrix-vector models for the 3D Ising CFT

- **arXiv**: [2611.12071](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12071)
- **Date**: 2026-11-09
- **Category**: complexity
- **Authors**: Kaichi Mizohata, Tatsuya Seko, Asato Tsuchiya
- **NOFX Relevance Score**: 1

## Abstract

The construction of three-dimensional conformal field theories (3D CFTs) using interacting fermions in the lowest Landau level on a sphere has recently proven remarkably efficient, while it differs from that of conventional field theories on the fuzzy sphere. Focusing on the 3D Ising CFT, we consider a class of models that are defined by deformed Hamiltonians specified by a function which controls how the interaction is damped at high angular momenta, and this class includes the original model. We show that each model in the class can be reformulated in a basis-independent manner as a matrix--vector quantum-mechanical model, and, upon integrating out the fermionic vectors, as a matrix model. We then focus on a Gaussian choice, for which the bosonic matrices acquire a heat-kernel propagator whose width is controlled by a parameter $ξ$. Through exact diagonalization with the number of fermions up to $16$, we find that the low-energy spectra of the deformed Hamiltonians at criticality agree with those in the 3D Ising CFT for a range of $ξ$. We thus obtain matrix-model realizations of the 3D Ising CFT that, in contrast to conventional fuzzy-sphere field theories, show no sign of UV/IR mixing in the low-lying spectrum.
