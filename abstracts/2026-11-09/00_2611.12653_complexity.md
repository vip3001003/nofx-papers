# A Hierarchy of Fast and Strong Signatures for Vertex Orbits

- **arXiv**: [2611.12653](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12653)
- **Date**: 2026-11-09
- **Category**: complexity
- **Authors**: Joe Germany, Mohamad Al Sayed, Rana Dbouk et al.
- **NOFX Relevance Score**: 0

## Abstract

The defining features of a physical system are its symmetries and its invariants. For a network, the symmetries are the automorphisms, and the invariants are the vertex orbits they define. Two vertices of a graph may appear structurally indistinguishable without being interchangeable under any graph automorphism. Distinguishing apparent similarity from genuine symmetry lies at the heart of the vertex-orbit partition problem. In this work, we develop a hierarchy of increasingly expressive, automorphism-invariant signatures that draw on the spectral properties of the graph Laplacian and the Dirac operator to reveal subtle structural differences. Guided by a sequence of counterexamples, we progressively strengthen these signatures, ultimately incorporating probing techniques, which are temporary structural perturbations, that expose the hidden distinctions in the original unperturbed graph. We evaluate our approach across diverse benchmark families, recovering exact orbit partitions in challenging instances, including graphs that resist low-dimensional Weisfeiler--Leman refinement. We also examine the computational and numerical limitations of our constructions, distinguishing empirical success from theoretical completeness. Our findings highlight the potential of spectral and probing-based methods for uncovering the structural distinctions that define vertex orbit.
