# Resetting-based designated information distribution

- **arXiv**: [2611.12485](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12485)
- **Date**: 2026-11-09
- **Category**: complexity
- **Authors**: Xiaojuan Ma, Yan Wang, Indranil Gupta
- **NOFX Relevance Score**: 0

## Abstract

For a network of $n$ nodes under random gossip dynamics, we show that by introducing the additional resetting mechanism (with rate $β$) to a designated subset of $r$ nodes, the expected sum of all node values is asymptotically equal to $\tfrac{n}{r}$ times the sum of the initial values of the $r$ designated nodes, in the small-$β$ and long-time limit. We also analyze the convergence speed to this asymptotic state and provide an approximate high-probability confidence interval for the expected sum at finite time $t$ and finite $β$. Operationally, we propose a protocol that distributes the initial node values over the designated subset evenly (up to a prescribed precision), by appropriately turning on and off the resetting dynamics. Our work not only offers insights into classical problems such as distributed computing and network control, but also serves as a potential foundation for designing quantum algorithms that prepare desired states in quantum networks using only local operations.
