# 基于阈值的持续优化选择：叶子脱落实例化

- **arXiv**: [2609.10588](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10588)
- **日期**: 2026-09-06
- **分类**: bio_inspired
- **作者**: Nasser Khalili
- **NOFX相关度**: 0

## 摘要

This paper formalizes threshold-based selection as an evaluation-gating architecture in which each incumbent is tested before variation and a replacement is generated and evaluated only when contextual pressure exceeds intrinsic strength.该机制被实例化为叶子脱落优化（LAO），使用基于等级的强度、物候季节信号、多样性调制、环境压力和基础再生内核。 A blocked two-to-the-fourth-power factorial analysis at dimension 10 on the CEC 2017 suite reduces the original multi-layer design to a parsimonious core: drift is harmful, while the other three auxiliary layers show no robust independent evidence of benefit.在相同的评估预算下，最终的 LAO-Core 在维度 10、30 和 50 的九个优化器中获得了第三好的平均 Friedman 排名。 A four-budget sweep shows budget-dependent relative performance, with adaptive differential-evolution baselines gaining relative advantage at larger budgets;九单元维度预算分析既没有建立仅针对每个维度的评价预算的法则，也没有建立统计上显着的维度预算相互作用。配对干预表明，多样性调制改变了后期运行的替换行为，而对测试预算的最终错误没有可检测到的影响。 The evidence supports LAO as a parsimonious evaluation-gating mechanism with regime-qualified competitiveness, rather than as a generally superior optimizer.
