# Rényi multi-entropy from Riemann surfaces

- **arXiv**: [2611.34715](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34715)
- **日期**: 2026-11-25
- **分类**: complexity
- **作者**: Kenya Tasuki
- **NOFX相关度**: 2

## 摘要

We compute vacuum tripartite Rényi multi-entropy in a two-dimensional compact-boson conformal field theory (CFT). For every integer Rényi index $n\geq2$, we evaluate the partition function on the replica Riemann surface for disjoint intervals, obtaining the twist four-point function in terms of a ratio of theta functions. An independent calculation of the partition function on a Fermat curve yields the adjacent three-point coefficient. In the fusion limit, the Riemann surface for disjoint intervals degenerates into two Fermat curves, and the residue of the four-point function reproduces the squared three-point coefficient, as required by the operator product expansion. The genuine multi-entropy therefore approaches twice its strictly adjacent value. The CFT predictions agree with lattice calculations for the critical XXZ and Ising spin chains and a harmonic chain. We also exhibit a unitary CFT in which the canonical replica construction yields complex Rényi multi-entropy.
