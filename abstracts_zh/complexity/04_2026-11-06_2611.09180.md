# Continuum mean-field theory of spatiotemporal pattern formation in a three-state active Potts model

- **arXiv**: [2611.09180](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09180)
- **日期**: 2026-11-06
- **分类**: complexity
- **作者**: Yuta Tateyama, Songhao Yin, Hiroaki Ito et al.
- **NOFX相关度**: 4

## 摘要

We investigate the spatiotemporal dynamics of a three-state active Potts model with a cyclic transition bias. Starting from microscopic Glauber-type dynamics, we derive macroscopic continuum field equations through a spatial mean-field approximation. Linear stability and weakly nonlinear analyses reveal that increasing the coupling strength induces a Hopf bifurcation, near which the critical amplitude dynamics is described by the complex Ginzburg--Landau equation. In the spatially homogeneous limit, the system exhibits limit-cycle oscillations and ordered states, with an intermediate regime of bistability bounded by a $\mathbb{Z}_3$-symmetric heteroclinic bifurcation. In two dimensions, the system sustains stable rotating spiral waves. Using perturbation theory based on one-dimensional traveling fronts, we semianalytically predict the curvature-dependent propagation velocity of circular fronts, as well as the rotational angular velocity and pitch of spiral waves. Finally, we compare the continuum model with discrete Monte Carlo simulations to clarify the role of thermal fluctuations and evaluate the validity of the mean-field approximation.
