# Non-Gaussian limit distributions of giant-component sizes in inhomogeneous networks

- **arXiv**: [2611.13683](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13683)
- **日期**: 2026-11-10
- **分类**: complexity
- **作者**: Tomoaki Nogawa, Takehisa Hasegawa
- **NOFX相关度**: 0

## 摘要

We demonstrate that the size distribution of giant components in percolation and epidemic spreading models on highly inhomogeneous networks often fails to converge to a Gaussian distribution in the thermodynamic limit. Instead, when appropriately scaled, the distribution converges to a non-Gaussian limit distribution (NGLD). This scaling behavior aligns with the generalized central limit theorem (GCLT), which governs sums of random variables following heavy-tailed distributions. For scale-free networks, the scaling exponent is determined solely by the degree exponent of the network, independently of the control parameter. In contrast, on the Farey graph, a deterministic hierarchical small-world network, the scaling exponent varies continuously with the control parameter.
