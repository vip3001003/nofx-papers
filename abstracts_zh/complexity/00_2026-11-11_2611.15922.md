# Monopole crystal and effective theory for a monopole-enhanced 3D U(1) gauge theory

- **arXiv**: [2611.15922](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15922)
- **日期**: 2026-11-11
- **分类**: complexity
- **作者**: Alessandro Mariani
- **NOFX相关度**: 0

## 摘要

Recently, a version of U(1) lattice gauge theory on the cubic lattice with a monopole enhancement term was considered in the literature. In this work, we further our understanding of this theory with a mixture of analytical and numerical methods. We show that for sufficiently large monopole enhancement, the monopoles are no longer dilute and they arrange themselves into an antiferromagnetic, Néel-ordered ground state. Nonetheless, a variant of Polyakov's argument for the dilute excitations over the dense ground state leads to an effective sine-Gordon model which is qualitatively similar to that of the standard theory. We also predict analytically the presence of first-order transitions as the monopole chemical potential is varied at fixed gauge coupling, corresponding to a change in the dominant monopole charge. Similarly, we predict a transition in the Ising universality class as the gauge coupling is varied. We then use numerical methods to study the phase diagram of the theory and check some of our predictions.
