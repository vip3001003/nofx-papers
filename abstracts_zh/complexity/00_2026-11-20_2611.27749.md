# Lattice-geometry dependence of Type-I and Type-II magnetic flux responses in strongly correlated bosons

- **arXiv**: [2611.27749](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27749)
- **日期**: 2026-11-20
- **分类**: complexity
- **作者**: Takayuki Yokoyama, Yasuhiro Tada
- **NOFX相关度**: 0

## 摘要

Superfluids and superconductors are classified into Type-I and Type-II according to their response to a magnetic flux, but this classification has hardly been addressed for lattice bosons under magnetic flux, where the Meissner and vortex phases have been studied mostly in ladder geometries. Here, we study the flux response of the extended Bose-Hubbard model with nearest-neighbor interaction $V$ at half filling on square and triangular ladders with two to four legs by using the density-matrix renormalization group. On the square ladders, the vortex phase is confined to a region near $V=0$. At finite $V$, a magnetic flux-induced charge-density-wave (CDW) order replaces it, and the response becomes Type-I-like. In contrast, on the triangular ladders, the vortex phase persists over the whole range of $V$ studied, and the response remains Type-II-like. By extrapolating the ladder results to two dimensions, we argue that the Type-I or Type-II character of the flux response depends on the lattice geometry, which suggests a role of geometric frustration.
