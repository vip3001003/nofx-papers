# Correlation functions of the Lieb--Liniger Bose gas in equilibrium states: a microscopic approach to space-time asymptotics

- **arXiv**: [2611.18316](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18316)
- **日期**: 2026-11-13
- **分类**: complexity
- **作者**: Frank Göhmann, Karol K. Kozlowski, Mikhail D. Minin
- **NOFX相关度**: 2

## 摘要

We develop an approximation-free approach to the analysis of form-factor series for the correlation functions of quantum integrable models in the thermodynamic limit and in equilibrium states described by a class of stationary density matrices. This allows us to obtain the long-time, large-distance asymptotics of the correlation functions in thermal equilibrium and in stationary states after a quench, including such states that can be described by generalized Gibbs ensembles. Our approach will be exemplified with the Lieb--Liniger Bose gas or non-linear Schrödinger model. It applies to the correlation functions of all local operators. For the two-point functions of particle-density or particle-current density operators our findings confirm the phenomenological predictions based on generalised hydrodynamics. We extract as well the long-time, large-distance asymptotics of a field- conjugated field correlation function. At present its behaviour is outside the reach of any heuristic method. It is governed by a novel non-linear integral equation that depends on space and time.
