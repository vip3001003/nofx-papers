# Emergent Symmetry in Constant-Density Flocks

- **arXiv**: [2611.07771](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07771)
- **日期**: 2026-11-05
- **分类**: complexity
- **作者**: Hugues Chaté, Alexandre Solon
- **NOFX相关度**: 0

## 摘要

In two dimensions, the ordered phase of constant-density flocks is described by a single angle field, which makes them arguably the simplest systems with a scale-free ordered phase of collective motion. We show that the equation governing the corresponding Nambu-Goldstone mode, completed with terms previously discarded, possesses on large scales an invariance combining a shear and a Galilean boost. Broken at the bare level, this symmetry is restored at the unique attractive fixed point, and the associated Ward identities fix the scaling exponents exactly, as confirmed numerically.
