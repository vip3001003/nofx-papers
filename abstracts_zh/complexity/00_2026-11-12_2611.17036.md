# Ergodicity breaking for data classification in a dissipative Ising model with competing two- and four-body interactions

- **arXiv**: [2611.17036](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17036)
- **日期**: 2026-11-12
- **分类**: complexity
- **作者**: Simon Kochsiek, Mario Boneberg, Igor Lesanovsky
- **NOFX相关度**: 0

## 摘要

We explore the close relationship between the processing of data and the dynamics of interacting many-body systems. For example, certain classes of quantum neural networks can be mapped onto dissipative quantum Ising models whose evolution is described by a Lindblad master equation. In these architectures, emergent nonlinearities lead to non-ergodic dynamics under which initial states located in different basins of attraction evolve towards distinguishable stationary states. This mechanism underlies the classification of quantum data. Motivated by this connection, we consider an Ising model with competing two- and four-body interactions. Using a combination of mean-field calculations and exact small-scale numerics, we show that the stationary state manifold can be designed to enable multi-class classification. We also investigate the structure of the ensuing basins of attraction, thereby showing that these systems allow for the classification of quantum states with nonlinear decision boundaries.
