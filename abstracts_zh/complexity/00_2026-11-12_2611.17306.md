# Statistics of Fock-space correlation lengths in the many-body localised phase

- **arXiv**: [2611.17306](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17306)
- **日期**: 2026-11-12
- **分类**: complexity
- **作者**: Bibek Saha, David E. Logan, Sthitadhi Roy
- **NOFX相关度**: 0

## 摘要

The presence of long-ranged, many-body resonances in the many-body localised phase of disordered quantum systems is manifest in strong inhomogeneities of eigenstate amplitudes on the Fock space. We develop a quantitative, microscopic theory for this, centred on two-point Fock-space correlation functions. Exact diagonalisation of the disordered tilted-field Ising chain uncovers a stark contrast: typical correlations decay perfectly exponentially, whereas average correlations exhibit subexponential decay with systematic system-size dependence driven by rare resonant nodes. We show that this behaviour stems from an underlying distribution of Fock-space correlation lengths with a variance that scales inversely with system size. Crucially, the interplay of this distribution with the high-dimensional geometry of the hypercubic Fock-space graph, leads to the qualitative difference between the typical and average behaviours. The theory also allows us to derive the singularity spectrum of the many-body localised eigenstates, and to establish theoretically the anomalous symmetry violation of quantum multifractality as an inherent consequence of the Fock-space geometry.
