# Diffusion Effects on a Stochastic Amplifier Driven by the Square of a Gaussian Field: Discontinuous Transition to Intermittency

- **arXiv**: [2611.34532](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34532)
- **日期**: 2026-11-25
- **分类**: complexity
- **作者**: Philippe Mounaix
- **NOFX相关度**: 1

## 摘要

We study the effects of diffusion on the transition to intermittency in stochastic amplification driven by the square of a Gaussian field. The transition is characterized by an order parameter quantifying the spatial uniformity of the amplification. We consider a model in which the diffusion-amplification process is embedded in a random lattice of point absorbers in such a way that the Feynman-Kac formula is restricted to Brownian paths with a range not exceeding a finite cut-off. In this setting, with the Gaussian driving field in a wide class of physical interest, we show that diffusion-induced damping of the amplification is sufficiently strong to change the nature of the transition: from continuous in the absence of diffusion, to discontinuous in the presence of diffusion.
