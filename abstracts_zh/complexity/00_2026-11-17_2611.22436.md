# Confinement Alters Energy Relaxation in Subrecoil Laser Cooling

- **arXiv**: [2611.22436](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22436)
- **日期**: 2026-11-17
- **分类**: complexity
- **作者**: Daiki Kanbe, Eli Barkai, Takuma Akimoto
- **NOFX相关度**: 0

## 摘要

Controlling atomic motion through laser cooling requires both reducing kinetic energy and confining atoms to a finite spatial region. Here, we investigate how harmonic confinement modifies the long-time energy relaxation in subrecoil laser cooling. Using a stochastic scattering-rate model, numerical simulations, and an analytically tractable renewal approximation, we show that confinement qualitatively changes the asymptotic relaxation law. For a scattering rate that vanishes at low momentum as $R(p)\propto |p|^α$, the change arises because confinement reshapes the phase-space geometry of rare, long trapping events. In free space, such events require only small momentum. Under harmonic confinement, however, position and momentum are coupled along phase-space orbits, restricting long-lived low-scattering trajectories to the vicinity of the phase-space origin. This geometric constraint modifies the trapping-time statistics, produces two dynamical crossovers, and leads to an optimal scattering-rate exponent of $α=4$ for the fastest asymptotic decay of the mean energy. Our results demonstrate that confinement changes the asymptotic energy-relaxation dynamics even when the local scattering law remains unchanged.
