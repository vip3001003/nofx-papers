# Genuinely Out-of-Equilibrium Bose-Einstein Condensation

- **arXiv**: [2611.26123](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26123)
- **日期**: 2026-11-19
- **分类**: complexity
- **作者**: Tatsuhiko N. Ikeda, Masahito Ueda
- **NOFX相关度**: 2

## 摘要

Bose-Einstein (BE) condensation typically occurs in the ground state. Prominent exceptions include excitons, whose ground state is the vacuum; consequently, they undergo BE condensation only in out-of-equilibrium regimes to counteract rapid decay. Here, we develop a driven-dissipative framework to address this challenge, demonstrating that BE condensates emerge under sufficiently strong excitation in one and two dimensions. In three dimensions, by incorporating effective loss rates and a specified injection energy, we find a percent-level condensate fraction, consistent in order of magnitude with the exciton experiment of Morita et al. (Nat. Commun. 13, 5388 (2022)).
