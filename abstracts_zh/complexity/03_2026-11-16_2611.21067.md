# The $N$-channel white-noise Schrödinger operator in all three Dyson classes. I. Explicit stationary admittance distribution, density of states and outgoing Kontsevich matrix Airy integrals

- **arXiv**: [2611.21067](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21067)
- **日期**: 2026-11-16
- **分类**: complexity
- **作者**: Yan V. Fyodorov
- **NOFX相关度**: 3

## 摘要

We obtain an explicit stationary distribution of the matrix Riccati variable---the boundary admittance---for an $N$-channel Schrödinger operator with Gaussian white-noise disorder, in all three Dyson symmetry classes $β=1,2,4$. Uniform absorption regularizes the Riccati dynamics, and the distribution's normalization factorizes into outgoing Kontsevich matrix Airy integrals. This gives a common route to the density of states and the sum of Lyapunov exponents, extending Halperin's scalar solution to coupled channels. Exact finite-$N$ formulas connect two spectral regimes: the Lifshitz tail, including its symmetry-dependent prefactor, and the collective large-$N$ edge. We reduce the $β=4$ and even-$N$ $β=1$ problems to unitary orthogonal polynomials and an auxiliary recursion. Their critical double-scaling limit yields edge profiles on the $N^{-4/5}$ energy scale, governed by a complex Painlevé-I tritronquée solution and a linear auxiliary equation. We conjecture that these profiles describe a universal edge regime of random band matrices.
