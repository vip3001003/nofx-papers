# Exact fermionic decay length and Majorana localization in the sign-alternating kagomé Ising model

- **arXiv**: [2611.26529](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26529)
- **日期**: 2026-11-19
- **分类**: complexity
- **作者**: Shahane Khachatryan, Hrachya Babujian, Ara SEdrakyan
- **NOFX相关度**: 1

## 摘要

We derive an exact fermionic decay length for the sign-alternating kagomé Ising model at fixed transverse momentum $p=π/2$. Writing $x=\tanh K_1$, $y=\tanh K_2$, and $z=\tanh K_3$ for the three dimensionless couplings, the inverse length per lattice cell is $ξ_{\rm f}^{-1}=|\ln(U/|V|)|$, where $U=(1-x^2)(1+y^2z^2)$ and $V=(1+x^2)(y^2-z^2)$. The length diverges at $U=|V|$, with the sign of $V$ selecting one of two classical critical branches. In a simultaneous strong- and weak-bond scaling, these branches become the gap-closing conditions at momenta $0$ and $π$ of the transverse-field Ising chain obtained from the row transfer matrix. On the topological side, the classical decay length tends to the localization length of a Majorana end mode of the Jordan--Wigner fermion chain. We also prove that the regular strong-bond Yang--Baxter family cannot be continued to the finite-coupling interior within the fixed-basis ansatz with one vanishing bond direction.
