# Topology Does Not Determine Defect Mobility in Active Polar Matter

- **arXiv**: [2611.04428](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04428)
- **日期**: 2026-11-03
- **分类**: complexity
- **作者**: Abhra Puitandy, Shradha Mishra
- **NOFX相关度**: 0

## 摘要

Topological defects are commonly characterized by their charge, although defects with the same charge can have different internal morphologies. We study the annihilation of isolated $\pm1$ defect pairs in a two-dimensional compressible Toner--Tu model, varying the morphology of the $+1$ defect while keeping the initial charges and separation fixed. Activity produces a morphology-dependent mobility asymmetry: reversing the radial orientation of an aster reverses which defect travels farther, and asters show the strongest response. An early-time analysis reveals that the initial morphology selects distinct density modes whose gradients subsequently feed back on the polarization. Consistently, removing the density-gradient coupling strongly suppresses the asymmetry. These results establish defect morphology, in addition to topological charge, as a dynamical variable governing defect-pair motion in active polar matter.
