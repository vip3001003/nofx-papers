# Memory-gated transient anomalous transport of quasiparticles out of equilibrium

- **arXiv**: [2611.14261](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14261)
- **日期**: 2026-11-10
- **分类**: complexity
- **作者**: Pranay Venkatesh, Tianchu Li, Srijan Bhattacharyya et al.
- **NOFX相关度**: 1

## 摘要

Anomalous transport is ubiquitous across condensed matter, materials science, and biophysics. Yet, in far-from-equilibrium settings, what determines when, how much, and in which direction transport departs from diffusion remains a fundamental open question. By adopting the dispersive Holstein model of small polarons as representative of a wide class of dissipative systems, we show that a signed bath memory time governs the direction and the strength of the transient departure, and that when it vanishes, diffusion emerges without an asymptotic delay. Tuning the dissipative environment from resonant to off-resonant with the carrier density of states, transport turns from subdiffusion to superdiffusion as the memory time changes sign. Microscopically, we trace this memory effect to a competition between polaron formation and coherent polaron motion. These findings establish a unified memory-based description of transient anomalous transport, offering a path to control nonequilibrium quasiparticle flow through environmental tuning.
