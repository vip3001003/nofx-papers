# Perfect Wave Transfer in Nonconformal Tomonaga-Luttinger Liquids

- **arXiv**: [2611.09018](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09018)
- **日期**: 2026-11-06
- **分类**: complexity
- **作者**: Davide Batic, Denys Dutykh
- **NOFX相关度**: 0

## 摘要

Perfect wave transfer (PWT) reproduces spatial reflection at a common finite time under a fixed Hamiltonian. We classify this process for open compact bosons and inhomogeneous Tomonaga--Luttinger liquids, distinguishing transfer of oscillator and neutral observables from transfer on the charged field algebra. In the single-charge model, the first two notions coincide. Global transfer additionally requires rephasing of the compact zero mode. For regular reflection-symmetric channels, exact oscillator transfer forces an odd multiple of the optical crossing time and a strictly ordered, parity-compatible spectrum differing from the conformal ladder at only finitely many levels. This spectral condition is also sufficient when the Robin boundary data induced by the physical profile are included. Requiring Neumann conditions after the Liouville transformation instead forces conformality. We construct an explicit, regular one-defect family for every $r>1/2$. Its transfer parameters are precisely the reduced fractions $r=p/q$ with odd numerator and denominator, with shortest transfer time $pL/v_0$. At the delay $3L/v_0$, the $r=3/5$ profile has numerical contrast $K_{\max}/K_{\min}\simeq 12.913$, compared with $81$ for $r=3$. Two independent conservative discretizations show second-order convergence on fixed mode bands. Occupation- and charge-constrained error estimates quantify robustness, while an exact homogeneous-chain calculation explains the $J^{2/3}$ resolved-band scale also observed in the engineered channels. These results provide a spectral design method and finite-device benchmarks for quadratic quantum channels. An interacting microscopic realization remains open.
