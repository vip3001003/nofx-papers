# Patterns and correlations in time-reversal symmetry breaking

- **arXiv**: [2611.34244](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34244)
- **日期**: 2026-11-25
- **分类**: complexity
- **作者**: Ido Patish, Mathias Casiulis, Dov Levine
- **NOFX相关度**: 3

## 摘要

Time-reversal symmetry (TRS) breaking is the hallmark of non-equilibrium statistical physics. This notwithstanding, in translationally invariant systems, it is almost always treated only as a global quantity. We propose to use the local entropy production rate as a spatio-temporal field to describe extended homogeneous non-equilibrium systems. We find that this field generically displays spatial correlations, with a typical length that grows as we approach some non-equilibrium phase transitions. We also show that the correlation length associated with the local entropy production rate follows heretofore unknown scaling laws involving control parameters as well as the observation time used for trajectories. Our work paves the way to the use of the local entropy production as a powerful observable to identify the scale and source of TRS breaking in non-equilibrium systems.
