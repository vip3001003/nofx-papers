# Microscopically exact transport equation for the quantum Calogero model

- **arXiv**: [2609.03018](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03018)
- **日期**: 2026-09-02
- **分类**: complexity
- **作者**: Vir B. Bulchandani, Fabian H. L. Essler
- **NOFX相关度**: 0

## 摘要

We derive an exact transport equation for the quantum Calogero model, i.e. inverse-square interacting bosons on a line, from microscopic first principles.
