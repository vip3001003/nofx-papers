# The Inverse Mean First-Passage Time Problem

- **arXiv**: [2611.03831](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03831)
- **日期**: 2026-11-02
- **分类**: complexity
- **作者**: Tommer D. Keidar, Shlomi Reuveni, Igor M. Sokolov
- **NOFX相关度**: 0

## 摘要

We consider the mean first-passage time $τ(x)$ for an overdamped particle in a one-dimensional potential landscape, and address the problem inverse to the textbook one: Given $τ(x)$ as a function of starting distance, we look for a potential that realizes it. We derive the conditions on $τ(x)$ under which such a potential exists. We show that while the power-law dependences $τ(x)\sim x^α$ with $α\leq 2$ may be realized by monotonic potentials, any realizable $τ(x)$ with superquadratic growth requires potential wells arbitrarily far from the target; $α= 2$ separates sliding from trapping-dominated motion regimes.
