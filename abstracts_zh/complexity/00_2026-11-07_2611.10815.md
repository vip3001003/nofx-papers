# Monodromy Defects and Bulk Renormalization Group Flows on the Fuzzy Sphere

- **arXiv**: [2611.10815](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.10815)
- **日期**: 2026-11-07
- **分类**: complexity
- **作者**: Kimyeong Lee, Joydeep Naskar
- **NOFX相关度**: 0

## 摘要

A monodromy around a defect specifies a symmetry transformation, but the low energy properties of the defect depend on its interaction with bulk degrees of freedom and also itself. We study this dependence on the fuzzy sphere. Along an anisotropy flow, an $O(3)$ half-twist approaches the $O(2)$ half-twist when its twisted components stay light and disappears when they become massive. At large $N$, untwisted spectators give a positive correction to the defect entropy, fixed by the conformal weight of the free defect. The surviving $O(2)$ monodromy admits a relevant charged perturbation that pins the line. Pinning before or after the easy-plane bulk flow gives matching infrared junction data. A pin of the untwisted component survives only the opposite, easy-axis flow. Walls between opposite pinnings test the stability of the candidate ordered line. They are relevant on the pinned $O(2)$ monodromy lines studied and favoured to be relevant, with a smaller margin, on the ordinary $O(2)$ line, while the ordinary $O(3)$ and $O(4)$ walls lie close to marginality. We separate the wall dimension into a tilt stiffness and an angular shape, comparing them with a Ward identity, a two-loop expansion and a large-$N$ saddle. In the fully twisted large-$N$ model, winding weakens the condensate and lowers the wall dimension and the entropy released by pinning. The resulting entropy comparison permits disordering into the unpinned line wherever the leading wall estimate is relevant. Finally, we construct the regular half-twist on the fuzzy four-sphere as a starting point for analogous questions in five dimensions.
