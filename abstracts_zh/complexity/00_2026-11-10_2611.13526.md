# A new representation of effective diffusion in fluctuating periodic potentials through stochastic duality

- **arXiv**: [2611.13526](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13526)
- **日期**: 2026-11-10
- **分类**: complexity
- **作者**: Derek Frydel
- **NOFX相关度**: 0

## 摘要

We revisit the classical problem of effective diffusion in fluctuating periodic force fields by combining homogenization with stochastic duality. We consider a specific class of fluctuations in which the internal state of the force field is independently redrawn from a fixed distribution at Poisson-distributed times. In the homogenization framework, the effective diffusion coefficient \(D_{\rm eff}\) is expressed in terms of a corrector function satisfying a backward Kolmogorov equation. For the redraw dynamics considered here, the equation governing the spatial derivative of the corrector can be reinterpreted as the stationary forward equation of a dual system obtained by reversing the force field, \(F\to-F\). This relation eliminates the corrector and yields a representation of \(D_{\rm eff}\) entirely in terms of stationary densities and probability currents of the physical and dual systems. We further introduce an auxiliary function \(Q(x)\) whose spatial variation is determined by the stationary current. When this current vanishes, \(Q(x)\) becomes independent of position and the effective diffusion coefficient reduces to the local relation \( D_{\rm eff}=L^2Q. \) This result generalizes the classical Lifson--Jackson relation to fluctuating periodic force fields. We verify the theory using analytical calculations, numerical solutions of the stationary equations, and direct simulations of the long-time particle displacement, including a driven system with nonzero stationary current.
