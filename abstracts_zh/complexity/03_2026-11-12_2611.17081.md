# Theory of critical behavior at non-polar structural phase transitions from functional renormalization

- **arXiv**: [2611.17081](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17081)
- **日期**: 2026-11-12
- **分类**: complexity
- **作者**: Max Fornoville, Kilian Fraboulet, Michael M. Scherer et al.
- **NOFX相关度**: 3

## 摘要

$O(N)$ models provide a versatile framework for describing continuous phase transitions in a wide variety of physical systems. We investigate an extension of these models arising from order parameters that transform under interlocked internal and spatial symmetries which describe transitions towards non-polar structural order. Such order occurs, for instance, in dipolar antiferromagnets, spin-orbit-coupled magnets, non-polar ferroics, and certain nematic systems. The resulting effective theory is an $O(d)$ model with an additional symmetry-allowed kinetic term $h(\partial_iφ_i)^2$, which modifies the relative stiffness of longitudinal and transverse fluctuations. We study both thermal and quantum phase transitions within this $O(d)$ model using the functional renormalization group (FRG). In contrast to conventional $O(N)$ models, the interlocking of internal and spatial symmetries distinguishes spatial and imaginary-time derivatives, such that the quantum model is not simply obtained from the thermal one by a dimensional extension $d\rightarrow d+1$. We derive and analyze the corresponding FRG flow equations, paying particular attention to the tensor structure of the two-point function, which is essential for consistently extracting the flow of the kinetic terms. Our analysis shows that the additional kinetic coupling is irrelevant at the $O(d)$ Wilson-Fisher fixed point in both thermal and quantum cases, where quantum-to-classical correspondence thus re-emerges. However, its small but sizable correction-to-scaling exponent gives rise to substantial corrections to scaling in the vicinity of the critical point.
