# Slow mixing of Gibbs sampling for the quantum Curie-Weiss model

- **arXiv**: [2611.34682](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34682)
- **日期**: 2026-11-25
- **分类**: complexity
- **作者**: Xiaoyang Huang
- **NOFX相关度**: 0

## 摘要

Hamiltonian evolution can spread local operators across a dense quantum magnet. We show that this global support does not remove the bottleneck to thermalization in the quantum Curie-Weiss model: at fixed low temperature and weak transverse field, the Chen-Kastoryano-Gilyen Gibbs sampler [arXiv: 2311.09207] has an exponentially large mixing-time lower bound. A dynamical quantum bottleneck theory that generalizes the classical notion of conductance has been proposed alongside existing frameworks that rely on spatial locality. We also give a complementary interpretation of slow mixing using an effective Fokker-Planck equation.
