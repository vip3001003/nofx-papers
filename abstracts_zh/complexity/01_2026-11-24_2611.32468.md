# Classifying exact local-equilibrium solutions of the Boltzmann equation

- **arXiv**: [2611.32468](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32468)
- **日期**: 2026-11-24
- **分类**: complexity
- **作者**: Nagi Khalil, Jorge Bravo-Gadea
- **NOFX相关度**: 1

## 摘要

We provide a complete classification, in a \(d\)-dimensional Euclidean space, of all hydrodynamic states and external forces for which a local equilibrium distribution constitutes an exact solution to the Boltzmann kinetic equation. We demonstrate that the temperature \(T\) must be spatially uniform, that the velocity field is the sum of a rigid translation, a radial expansion or compression, and a rigid rotation, and that the external acceleration must be conservative, though time-dependent within a reference frame that translates and rotates along with the fluid. The density profile is then determined by the external potential, while its temporal dependence is governed by any temperature function \(T(t)\) that can be generated through an appropriate adjustment of the external acceleration. We recover the classic ``harmonic breather'' and obtain solutions involving static forces that exhibit algebraic cooling, similar to Haff's law, despite the occurrence of elastic collisions. Our classification identifies the exceptional circumstances under which local equilibrium is exact and provides rigorous criteria for evaluating kinetic and hydrodynamic approximations.
