# Noise-induced manifolds: reduction of slow--fast SDEs to switching processes

- **arXiv**: [2611.20903](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.20903)
- **日期**: 2026-11-16
- **分类**: complexity
- **作者**: Jeremy R. Worsfold, Patrick Ryan, Richard G. Morris
- **NOFX相关度**: 0

## 摘要

When constituents copy one another, as in reaction networks, collective behaviour and opinion dynamics, the noise of copying vanishes at consensus and can make a system bistable. More generally this noise vanishes on whole manifolds, along which slower variables keep moving and set the switching rates, yet no general method derives these switching dynamics. Here we reduce slow--fast stochastic differential equations whose fast noise acts in a single direction to a switching diffusion or piecewise-deterministic Markov process (PDMP), using the absorption probabilities of the fast noise in place of an invariant measure, and derive a first-order correction for the finite duration of switches. For the two-species Togashi--Kaneko model the reduction recovers our earlier PDMP, and the correction captures how the stationary composition depends on the timescale separation. In a model of foraging ants with a depleting food source, the hazard of leaving a source rises through every stay, which makes the colony unlikely to leave a source soon after arriving there.
