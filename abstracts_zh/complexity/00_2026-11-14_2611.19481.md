# Computing rare events in Lévy-driven systems via optimal control

- **arXiv**: [2611.19481](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.19481)
- **日期**: 2026-11-14
- **分类**: complexity
- **作者**: Jinan Pan, Timo Schorlepp
- **NOFX相关度**: 0

## 摘要

We extend the optimal control formulation of sample path large deviation theory, which has proven effective for numerical computation of instantons and sharp probability estimates in stochastic differential equations with Gaussian noise, to systems with light-tailed Lévy noise. In the scaling limit of small jumps and high intensity, such systems satisfy a large deviation principle of Freidlin-Wentzell type, with a running cost given by the Legendre transform of the noise cumulant generating function. We propose to numerically minimize this cost over noise realizations subject to the system dynamics, which yields the instanton without having to solve Hamilton's equations, and naturally handles degenerate and multiplicative noise. When the cumulant generating function has a bounded domain, the cost grows only linearly for large noise, and instantons can contain jumps. We analyze when jumps are not only admissible but optimal, depending on the subleading behavior of the cost and on the drift, and show how jumps can be resolved numerically through a time reparameterization that freezes physical time. For continuous instantons, we derive Riccati and Fredholm determinant expressions for the leading-order prefactor, and we discuss the modifications needed when the instanton jumps. We illustrate and validate our methods against Monte Carlo simulations in several examples: a two-dimensional nonlinear model, an active run-and-tumble particle with non-Gaussian tumbling noise, and the Ghil-Sellers energy balance climate model with Lévy-perturbed solar forcing.
