# Bypassing the Entropy Production Speed Limit of State-to-State Transitions with Adaptive Resetting

- **arXiv**: [2611.22712](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22712)
- **日期**: 2026-11-17
- **分类**: complexity
- **作者**: Daniel B. K. Chu, Rémi Goerlich, Itamar Steinitz et al.
- **NOFX相关度**: 0

## 摘要

State-to-state transitions in colloids and cold atoms (trap compression, bit erasure) are often achieved through driving, whose speed and entropy production are constrained by a fundamental bound. We show that this bound can be surpassed by changing the transport mechanism. Adaptive resetting enables transitions through nonlocal jumps, with rates learned from reset-free trajectories. A hybrid protocol, combining resetting with a switch to the target potential, surpasses the diffusive bound in three transformations. Finally, we derive a speed limit governing coupled diffusion-reset dynamics.
