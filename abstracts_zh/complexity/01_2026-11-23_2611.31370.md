# Ground-State Chiral Quantum Clusters

- **arXiv**: [2611.31370](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31370)
- **日期**: 2026-11-23
- **分类**: complexity
- **作者**: Matteo Massaro, Adolfo del Campo
- **NOFX相关度**: 1

## 摘要

Quantum time crystals are states of matter that spontaneously break time translation symmetry, extending the notion of crystalline order to time. While their out-of-equilibrium version has been successfully realized by periodic driving, the equilibrium counterpart remains elusive. Here we introduce "ground-state chiral quantum clusters": self-bound many-body ground states supporting a persistent current. We provide a minimal field theory describing these states, and an exact microscopic realization via the attractive anyonic Lieb-Liniger model on a ring. In the latter, the ground-state density-density correlator exhibits the Talbot effect, with a soliton-like profile that periodically disperses and revives while drifting indefinitely along the ring, realizing persistent motion at equilibrium. Possible experimental probes are discussed.
