# Geodesics and a Hubble Relation in Nonstationary Thermodynamic Geometry

- **arXiv**: [2611.19524](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.19524)
- **日期**: 2026-11-14
- **分类**: complexity
- **作者**: Wanyan Chen, Yu-Han Ma
- **NOFX相关度**: 1

## 摘要

Thermodynamic geometry offers a versatile lens from equilibrium to nonequilibrium, yet typically assumes a stationary metric. Here we study nonstationary thermodynamic geometry, where the metric evolves even at fixed control parameters. The resulting temporal connection generalizes the geodesic equation and redistributes dissipation along optimal control paths. We further establish a thermodynamic Hubble relation that distinguishes relaxation toward equilibrium from geometric expansion or contraction. Brownian examples demonstrate that externally modulated friction can reduce minimum dissipation, while heat exchange with a finite reservoir can induce geometric contraction and Mpemba-type relaxation. Our framework lays a geometric foundation for understanding complex evolving nonequilibrium systems.
