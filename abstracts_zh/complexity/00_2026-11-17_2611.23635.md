# Kinetic theory of dry granular particles under simple shear: steady flows and relaxation dynamics

- **arXiv**: [2611.23635](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23635)
- **日期**: 2026-11-17
- **分类**: complexity
- **作者**: Shunsuke Iizuka, Satoshi Takada, Hisao Hayakawa
- **NOFX相关度**: 0

## 摘要

We develop a kinetic theory for dry granular particles under simple shear flow using the Enskog approximation combined with Grad's moment method for moderately dense granular gases with the volume fraction $\varphi\le 0.5$. The collisional moments and stresses are expressed in terms of generalized hypergeometric functions, retaining all orders in the dimensionless shear-rate parameter within the adopted Grad closure. Within this framework, we derive a complete set of equations governing the kinetic stress and the collisional contribution to the stress tensor. The steady-state solutions reproduce the shear viscosity over a broad parameter range and improve the predictions of the kinetic temperature relative to the anisotropic Gaussian theory at the parameter values examined. Temperature still shows appreciable deviations under strongly inelastic conditions. Additionally, the theory captures the overall trends of the normal stress differences, while their quantitative accuracy depends on density and restitution coefficient. We further examine relaxation following an instantaneous reduction in $\varphi$ from $0.35$ to $0.20$ at a restitution coefficient of $0.90$. The theory reproduces the temperature and viscosity relaxation for this protocol.
