# Finite-size scaling of $\mathcal{PT}$-symmetry breaking decoupled from thermodynamic criticality in a complex-conjugate Ising model

- **arXiv**: [2611.19386](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.19386)
- **日期**: 2026-11-14
- **分类**: complexity
- **作者**: Yihan Wang, Yuting Wang, Lihui Sun et al.
- **NOFX相关度**: 3

## 摘要

We study a two-dimensional anisotropic Ising model with complex-conjugate couplings. Analytic continuation of the exact anisotropic Ising critical condition yields a candidate thermodynamic critical line, which is independently supported by finite-size scaling confirming the two-dimensional Ising universality class. Exact diagonalization of the transfer matrix and finite-size extrapolation reveal that the spectrum becomes complex, defining a separate $\mathcal{PT}$-symmetry-breaking boundary in the thermodynamic limit. We find $K_{\rm PT}<K_c$ throughout the parameter range studied, leaving an intermediate regime with a complex spectrum while the system remains thermodynamically disordered. The spatial correlations remain short-ranged at $K_{\rm PT}$, in contrast to the critical correlations at $K_c$, directly distinguishing the spectral transition from thermodynamic criticality. The complex-conjugate anisotropy modifies the finite-size free-energy amplitude, and accounting for the corresponding anisotropy factor recovers the Ising central charge $c=1/2$. These results demonstrate that spectral $\mathcal{PT}$-symmetry breaking can occur without thermodynamic criticality, establishing a decoupling between spectral and thermodynamic transitions in a two-dimensional non-Hermitian statistical system.
