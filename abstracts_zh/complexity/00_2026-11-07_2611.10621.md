# Dynamic Scaling and Aging in Hydrodynamic Coarsening of Vapor Bubbles in a Lennard-Jones Fluid

- **arXiv**: [2611.10621](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.10621)
- **日期**: 2026-11-07
- **分类**: complexity
- **作者**: Sutapa Roy
- **NOFX相关度**: 0

## 摘要

We investigate the coarsening and aging dynamics of vapor bubbles during liquid-vapor phase separation in a three-dimensional single-component Lennard-Jones fluid using large-scale molecular dynamics simulations. Following a temperature quench from a homogeneous high-temperature state into the two-phase coexistence region, vapor bubbles nucleate and grow in the liquid background. The characteristic bubble size follows a power-law growth, $\ell(t)\sim t^α$, with a growth exponent $α\simeq 2/3$, characteristic of inertial hydrodynamic coarsening. We also investigate the two-time autocorrelation function $C_{ag}(t,t_w)$ which follows waiting-time dependent relaxation, revealing aging. Furthermore, data at different waiting times collapse onto a scaling function when plotted as a function of the length ratio $\ell(t)/\ell(t_w)$, demonstrating dynamical scaling during aging. The corresponding scaling function exhibits an algebraic decay with an exponent $λ\simeq 3$. Interestingly, bubble and droplet growth exhibit distinctly different domain-growth and aging exponents despite both being disconnected morphologies.
