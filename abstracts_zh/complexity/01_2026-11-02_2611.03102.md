# The normal approximation to macroscopic dynamical fluctuations

- **arXiv**: [2611.03102](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03102)
- **日期**: 2026-11-02
- **分类**: complexity
- **作者**: Christian Maes, Kateřina Mladá, Karel Netočný
- **NOFX相关度**: 1

## 摘要

Under local detailed balance, the Lagrangian of macroscopic dynamical fluctuations has a canonical structure: its time-antisymmetric part is the entropy flux, and its time-symmetric part is a Legendre pair of kinetic and dissipation functions. We show what survives of that structure for normal (Gaussian) fluctuations around a nonequilibrium steady state. The quadratic Lagrangian keeps the canonical form, with the Hessian of the dissipation function as noise matrix. However, the force on the fluctuations differs from the linearized thermodynamic force by a term proportional to the driving and to the state dependence of the dynamical activity, so that local detailed balance is violated at the Gaussian level. Nevertheless, the Gaussian dynamics is gradient flow for the quadratic part of the nonequilibrium entropy, around an emergent nondissipative current. Finally, we show which parts of the Lagrangian can be reconstructed from measured Gaussian fluctuations.
