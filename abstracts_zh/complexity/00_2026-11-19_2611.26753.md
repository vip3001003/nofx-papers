# Geometric properties of Wilsonian flows of periodic potentials in two dimensions

- **arXiv**: [2611.26753](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26753)
- **日期**: 2026-11-19
- **分类**: complexity
- **作者**: Daniel G. Barci, Nathan O. Silvano
- **NOFX相关度**: 0

## 摘要

Periodic interactions can possess multiple competing minima and barriers, whose evolution under coarse-graining offers a geometric perspective complementary to the scaling of Fourier harmonics. We study this evolution in two dimensions by deriving a functional Wilsonian renormalization-group flow for a general smooth periodic potential and its running sine-Gordon frequency within a constant-kinetic projection. Reaction--diffusion and Burgers representations reveal two key properties of the flow: discrete translation symmetries are preserved, and the number of extrema can only remain constant or decrease. Consequently, the nonlinear generation of Fourier harmonics may accompany a progressive simplification of the potential landscape. We illustrate the role of symmetry with two complementary examples. A weak breaking of the $π$-translation symmetry allows a reduction from four extrema to two, whereas a non-sinusoidal interaction retaining this symmetry evolves from eight extrema to the four protected by its periodicity. These results highlight how symmetry and coarse-graining jointly reorganize competing minima and barriers in periodic interactions.
