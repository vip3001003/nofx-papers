# Entanglement Asymmetry and the Quantum Mpemba Effect in Inhomogeneous Quenches

- **arXiv**: [2611.32934](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32934)
- **日期**: 2026-11-24
- **分类**: complexity
- **作者**: Maëlle Olivier, Filiberto Ares, Pasquale Calabrese
- **NOFX相关度**: 1

## 摘要

Entanglement asymmetry is a powerful probe of symmetry breaking and its dynamical restoration following quantum quenches. While extensively studied for homogeneous initial states, its evolution from inhomogeneous initial configurations remains largely unexplored. We investigate a spin-$1/2$ XX chain whose two halves are initially prepared in tilted ferromagnetic states with different tilt angles. Combining generalized hydrodynamics (GHD) and the quasiparticle picture with exact fermionic correlation-matrix calculations, we study the dynamics of entanglement asymmetry. We find good agreement between the two approaches for generic initial configurations and observe the quantum Mpemba effect, whereby states with stronger initial symmetry breaking can restore the symmetry faster. This effect now depends on the subsystem position: while the homogeneous behavior is recovered when the subsystem lies entirely within one of the initially tilted halves, the entanglement-asymmetry curves for subsystems straddling the interface can exhibit multiple crossings or no crossings at all. A notable exception occurs for subsystems entirely within an initially non-tilted half. In this case, GHD predicts vanishing asymmetry, but the exact dynamics generates a small, nonzero asymmetry that can develop a plateau before decaying toward zero. This feature reveals contributions beyond the leading-order hydrodynamic description.
