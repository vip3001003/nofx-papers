# 胶体晶体发现的探索性数字炼金术

- **arXiv**: [2606.13586](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.13586)
- **日期**: 2026-06-11
- **分类**: complexity
- **作者**:  Shih-Kuang,  Lee, Sun-Ting Tsai et al.
- **NOFX相关度**: 0

## 摘要

Digital Alchemy (DA), introduced by Van Anders et al., is a statistical mechanics-based generalized thermodynamic ensemble method that employs computer simulations to optimize colloidal particle design.这种方法应用统计力学原理来预测和定制颗粒属性，从而产生所需的自组装结构或材料特性。 However, as an inverse design method, its main limitation is that the target structure must be known \textit{a priori}. Therefore, the optimal design from DA does not guarantee the targeted structure is the most or the only stable one. This highlights the importance of forward design with an exploratory scheme for optimizing novel colloid designs, which becomes more suitable in such cases.在本文中，我们介绍了探索性数字炼金术（EDA），这是一种增强的正向设计方案，首先从 DA 中释放目标晶体的约束，然后是探索导向的偏差，该偏差已广泛应用于元动力学（MetaD）等增强采样方法中。我们通过涉及粒子通过二维 Lennard-Jones 高斯势 (LJGP) 和三维振荡对势 (OPP) 相互作用的示例来展示 EDA 的实用性。 We applied EDA to study the free energy landscapes given different potential parameters of LJGP at different temperatures.通过探索性方案，我们还成功确定了一系列稳定亚稳态 Frank-Kasper 相的 OPP 电位参数。我们的方法将标准 DA 框架与元动力学融合在一起，这可能有助于研究广义系综中的炼金反应。
