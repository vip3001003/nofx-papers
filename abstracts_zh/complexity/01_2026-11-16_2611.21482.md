# Renormalization group study of critical behavior of $O(m)$ model with cubic anisotropy and extended defects: two-loop approximation

- **arXiv**: [2611.21482](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21482)
- **日期**: 2026-11-16
- **分类**: complexity
- **作者**: D. Shapoval, M. Dudka
- **NOFX相关度**: 1

## 摘要

We study the impact of quenched extended defects correlated in $ε_d$ dimensions and randomly distributed in the remaining $d - ε_d$ dimensions on the critical properties of a $d$-dimensional $O(m)$-symmetric system with cubic anisotropy. Exploiting the field-theoretic renormalization-group approach within the minimal subtraction scheme, we compute the two-loop renormalization-group functions. Applying resummation techniques to analyze the asymptotic series, we estimate the marginal values of $ε_d$ above which disorder becomes relevant for the physically important cases $m = 2$ and $m = 3$ in $d = 3$. We report numerical values of the critical exponents for the disorder-induced universality classes.
