# Noise-induced Extinction in Hierarchical Food Webs

- **arXiv**: [2611.33569](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33569)
- **日期**: 2026-11-25
- **分类**: complexity
- **作者**: Varun Nevash, Eric Forgoston, M. Ani Hsieh
- **NOFX相关度**: 1

## 摘要

Every finite population is eventually driven to extinction by demographic noise, even when the deterministic dynamics have a stable coexistence state. We develop a large-deviation theory of this process for hierarchical food webs of arbitrary size. In a community of $N$ species, the basal species $X_1$ reproduces intrinsically and every consumer may prey on all species below it, so that omnivory is built into the interaction graph. For unit conversion efficiency and self-limitation confined to the basal species, the mean-field dynamics have at most $2^{N-1}+1$ feasible fixed points. We prove that the sign of each passive eigenvalue equals the sign of the extinct species' density in the enlarged community, that every partial community containing $X_1$ is a saddle with one unstable direction for each extinct consumer, and that the coexistence state is the only stable fixed point. A WKB treatment of the master equation gives a $2N$-dimensional Hamiltonian whose fluctuational fixed points are obtained in closed form. The resulting extinction network has $3^{N-1}$ fluctuation-driven transitions, and its coarse-grained dynamics yield an exact recursion for every mean extinction time. We analyse the three-species omnivory module in full, including a minimal set of existence conditions, global stability of coexistence, numerical instanton actions for all nine transitions and the most probable extinction route, which we confirm with stochastic simulations. Finally, we show how deleting a single trophic link reshapes the extinction network.
