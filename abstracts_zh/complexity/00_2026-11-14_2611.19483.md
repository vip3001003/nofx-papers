# Bright-Dark State Interplay Governs Vibronic Decoherence in Molecular Aggregates

- **arXiv**: [2611.19483](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.19483)
- **日期**: 2026-11-14
- **分类**: complexity
- **作者**: Sreeja Loho Choudhury, Abhradeep Sarkar, Raja Ghosh
- **NOFX相关度**: 0

## 摘要

In this study, we establish a spectroscopy-driven framework that connects optically bright vibronic eigenstates to their subsequent decoherence dynamics in excitonic molecular aggregates. Combining the Multiparticle Holstein Formalism with explicit system--bath interactions, we demonstrate that relaxation-induced purity loss is governed by three interconnected factors: (i) the bath spectral window, which determines the accessibility of intra- and interband relaxation pathways; (ii) the local network of dark vibronic states surrounding the optically prepared bright state; and (iii) the energetic placement of the bright state, which controls downhill relaxation. Remarkably, states with different excitation energies exhibit nearly identical purity dynamics when their local relaxation networks are similar. The resulting H--J asymmetry further reveals the importance of energetic directionality. These findings establish a reliable connection between optical spectroscopy, vibronic band structure, and state-selective loss in purity.
