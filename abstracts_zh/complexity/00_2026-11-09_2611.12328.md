# The role of preparation protocols in the Mpemba effect under time-delayed Newton cooling

- **arXiv**: [2611.12328](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12328)
- **日期**: 2026-11-09
- **分类**: complexity
- **作者**: Andrés Santos
- **NOFX相关度**: 0

## 摘要

The Mpemba effect depends on both the relaxation dynamics and the protocol used to prepare the initial states. Here, this dependence is investigated using the time-delayed Newton cooling law, comparing protocols in which two samples are exposed to piecewise-constant bath temperatures before being placed in a common final bath. For protocols with at most two quenches and a common waiting time, exact conditions for the occurrence of the effect are derived. They rule out the Pontus protocol within this model and show that the synchronous two-reservoir protocol maximizes the effect magnitude within the class considered. The robustness of this optimum is then examined by allowing asynchronous quenches or an additional quench for one sample. Numerical optimization of these extensions identifies the synchronous two-reservoir protocol as the maximizing limit in the cases studied. This evidence motivates a conjecture that the same protocol remains optimal for more general bath histories, although that broader statement is not proved.
