# Real-Space-RG for the drawups and drawdowns of run-and-tumble trajectories: universality classes for models with space-dependent velocities and switching rates

- **arXiv**: [2611.18270](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18270)
- **日期**: 2026-11-13
- **分类**: complexity
- **作者**: Cecile Monthus
- **NOFX相关度**: 0

## 摘要

For one-dimensional run-and-tumble processes with the two velocities $v_{\pm}(x)$ and the two switching rates $γ_{\pm}(x)$, a given stochastic trajectory is an alternating sequence of local minima and local maxima corresponding to the switching events between the ballistic motions with velocities $v_{\pm}(x) $ in the two directions. The renormalized trajectory at scale $Γ$ is obtained by keeping only the alternating sequence of local minima and local maxima that are separated by drawups and drawdowns of amplitude bigger than $Γ$. Its statistical properties can be analyzed via the generalization of the previous work arxiv:2611.04853 concerning diffusion processes involving arbitrary diffusion coefficients $D(x)$ and Ito forces $F_I(x)$. The main result is that the scale function $s(x)$ of diffusion processes that depends only on the ratio $\frac{F_I(x)}{D(x) } $ is replaced by a pair of scale functions $s_{\pm}(x)$ that depend only on the two ratios $\frac{γ_+( x ) }{v_+( x )}$ and $\frac{γ_-( x ) }{v_-( x )}$. This pair of scale functions $s_{\pm}(x)$ govern the exit probabilities from an interval and determine the universality classes of the renormalized trajectories: all run-and-tumble processes with the same two ratios $\frac{γ_+( x ) }{v_+( x )}$ and $\frac{γ_-( x ) }{v_-( x )}$ share the same statistics of renormalized trajectories. This general framework is illustrated with examples that are compared to their diffusive counterparts : uniform symmetric and asymmetric processes on the whole line $x \in]-\infty,+\infty[$, geometric process on the half-line $x \in]0,+\infty[$, and an exponential functional of the telegraph process with a normalizable steady state.
