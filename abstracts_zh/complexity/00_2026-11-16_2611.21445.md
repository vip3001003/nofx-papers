# Localization transition in a spin chain with an exact Fock-space tree

- **arXiv**: [2611.21445](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21445)
- **日期**: 2026-11-16
- **分类**: complexity
- **作者**: Bernhard Jobst, Simone Warzel, Alexander Altland et al.
- **NOFX相关度**: 0

## 摘要

Many-body localization is often pictured as Anderson localization on the graph of many-body configurations. Strongly correlated disorder and a dense net of loops, however, make this graph hard to analyze. We introduce a local disordered spin-1 chain in which a single domain wall explores an exact binary tree of configurations. Random fields create strongly correlated disorder on this tree, as in generic interacting systems, while the absence of loops keeps the problem tractable. We rigorously bound the spreading of the domain wall at strong disorder, and efficient numerics on exponentially large trees indicate a localization transition at finite disorder strength.
