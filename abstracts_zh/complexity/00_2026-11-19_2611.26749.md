# Fully Automated Discovery of Phase Diagrams Using Agentic Large Language Models

- **arXiv**: [2611.26749](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26749)
- **日期**: 2026-11-19
- **分类**: complexity
- **作者**: Robert Amelung, Maximilian Nägele, Florian Marquardt
- **NOFX相关度**: 0

## 摘要

Classifying phases of matter and mapping out phase diagrams are central problems in physics. Recently, machine learning techniques have emerged as promising tools for phase classification, ranging from supervised learning to various unsupervised methods. However, these techniques provide limited qualitative insight into the discovered phases, typically work only for specific system classes, and do not by themselves efficiently map out the phase diagram. Autonomously constructing the phase diagram of a general system purely from measurements, without prior knowledge of the system, while also yielding qualitative understanding of the phases, remains an open task. In this work, we introduce Phasonaut, a two-agent framework that leverages the tool-use capabilities and broad knowledge of large language models (LLMs) to drive an active learning loop that constructs phase diagrams of physical systems and provides interpretations of the discovered phases. Starting from scratch, it iteratively selects and performs measurements, analyzes the resulting data, and segments the parameter domain into the phases it discovers. Phasonaut recovers the phase diagrams of classical and quantum spin systems to substantial precision. In the future, this framework could enable rapid exploration of systems with unknown phase diagrams, including in autonomously driven experiments.
