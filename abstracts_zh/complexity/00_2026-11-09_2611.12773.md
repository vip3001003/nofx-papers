# An efficient classical algorithm for OTOCs in chaotic quantum dynamics

- **arXiv**: [2611.12773](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12773)
- **日期**: 2026-11-09
- **分类**: complexity
- **作者**: Yunchao Liu, Yihui Quek, Shreya Vardhan et al.
- **NOFX相关度**: 0

## 摘要

Estimating out-of-time-ordered correlators (OTOCs) in individual instances of random quantum circuits has been proposed as a candidate for verifiable quantum advantage, and demonstrated to be hard for conventional classical algorithms such as tensor networks and Monte Carlo. Using insights from quantum chaos, we develop and implement a polynomial-time classical algorithm for the four-point ${\rm OTOC}^{(1)}$ and the eight-point ${\rm OTOC}^{(2)}$ in typical instances of one-dimensional (1D) quantum circuits. Our results demonstrate average-case classical tractability of estimating OTOCs in the sensitive regime where circuit-to-circuit fluctuations are large. Our algorithm organizes the OTOC into a rapidly converging series, where the leading term includes contributions from histories within permutation degrees of freedom in the replicated Hilbert space, and subleading terms arise from excursions outside this space. Although chaos is often viewed as an insurmountable barrier to classical simulation, our algorithm exploits a form of dephasing caused by chaos, which suppresses the subleading terms. We provide strong theoretical and numerical evidence to support this suppression. Further, we demonstrate that practical implementations of our algorithm estimate ${\rm OTOC}^{(1)}$ and ${\rm OTOC}^{(2)}$ in 1D circuits with high accuracy, achieving higher signal-to-noise ratio at larger circuit depths compared to recent 2D experiments by Google. Extending our algorithm to higher dimensions, and resolving the status of quantum advantage in 2D OTOCs, remain open problems.
