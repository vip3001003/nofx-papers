# Blanket theory for dense networks

- **arXiv**: [2611.23960](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23960)
- **日期**: 2026-11-18
- **分类**: complexity
- **作者**: Simone Franchini
- **NOFX相关度**: 2

## 摘要

We show how Blanket theory provides a constructive method for calculating cavity functionals in dense spin networks. Building on the description through interfaces and magnetization shells, we develop the calculation from the low-temperature fluctuations around the ground state. In this regime, dilute defects reveal the mechanism of random energy model (REM) universality for linear energies, while their sharing and renewal across layers organize the energies and Gibbs weights into a hierarchy. Because the same configurations determine the covariances of the fresh cavity fields, this construction identifies the fields and weights with an Aizenman-Sims-Starr random overlap structure. The cavity contributions can then be evaluated through recursive fractional means, yielding an explicit free-energy functional parametrized by layer magnetizations and hierarchical exponents, with coefficients determined by the interaction variances and the network normalization. Under the stated moment and normalization conditions, comparison of pressures transfers the limiting mean pressure from the Gaussian representative to networks with independent centered non-Gaussian couplings having the same variances. We illustrate the method with the deterministic Curie-Weiss model, recover the Parisi functional for the Sherrington-Kirkpatrick model, and derive the functionals for restricted and deep Boltzmann machines, including the full replica symmetry breaking (fullRSB) limit.
