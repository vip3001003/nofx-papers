# Free energy distribution for two non-crossing directed polymers on the cylinder

- **arXiv**: [2611.23981](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23981)
- **日期**: 2026-11-18
- **分类**: complexity
- **作者**: Andrea De Luca, Pierre Le Doussal
- **NOFX相关度**: 0

## 摘要

We consider the partition sum $Z_L(t)$ of two non-crossing continuum directed polymers of length $t$, in a common random medium with the geometry of a cylinder of circumference length $L$. In the limit $t \to +\infty$, the $k$-th cumulant of (minus) the free energy $\log Z_L(t)$, grows as $c_k(L) t$, and we compute the amplitudes $c_k(L)$. To this aim we first study the integer moments $\langle Z_L(t)^n \rangle$, whose long-time behavior is determined by the ground-state energy of the one-dimensional attractive Lieb--Liniger model in the symmetry sector associated with the Young diagram $(n,n)$ (a two-component bosonic nested Bethe ansatz). This ground state energy is obtained using the nested Bethe-Ansatz involving $2n$ physical rapidities and $n$ auxiliary rapidities. The analytic continuation in the replica number $n$, which yields the cumulants $c_k(L)$, is performed through a non-trivial generalization of the method developed by Brunet and Derrida for a single polymer on the cylinder. We obtain fully explicit formulas for the mean $c_1(L)$ and the variance $c_2(L)$, and construct a systematic scheme for computing all higher cumulants $c_k(L)$. Finally, taking $L\to+\infty$ and $n\to0$ with $nL^{1/2}$ fixed, we derive a universal scaling function which characterizes the large-deviation statistics of the two-polymer free energy and is expected to be universal within the one-dimensional two-layer periodic SHE/KPZ class. The two tails of the large-deviation function are obtained analytically, revealing different roles of the non-crossing constraint in favorable and unfavorable fluctuations of the common disorder.
