# Upper and lower bounds for the critical point of the three-dimensional Ising model

- **arXiv**: [2611.31872](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31872)
- **日期**: 2026-11-24
- **分类**: complexity
- **作者**: Zhongpu Qiu
- **NOFX相关度**: 1

## 摘要

We prove that the critical point $K_c$ of the nearest-neighbour Ising ferromagnet on $\mathbb{Z}^3$ satisfies $0.2164203058<K_c<0.2293910295$, equivalently $1.541630483<e^{2K_c}<1.582145851$, with both endpoints given by explicit rational numbers. To our knowledge these are the sharpest rigorous bounds available. They improve the classical bounds of Fisher (1967) and of Fröhlich, Simon and Spencer (1976), narrowing the rigorously known interval by a factor of about 3.5, and the interval contains the numerical estimate of the critical point. For the lower bound we refine Fisher's self-avoiding-walk argument. A shortest path in the high-temperature expansion forbids all bonds spanned by its vertices, and the resulting gain is estimated through local correlation integrals and summed over paths by a finite-memory transfer bound. As a by-product, the connective constant of $\mathbb{Z}^3$ is shown to be at most $4.73195$. For the upper bound we prove an infrared bound whose coefficient involves the nearest-neighbour correlation. We keep the zero Fourier mode as an unknown and force it to be positive by exact local relations for the spins on a small shell. The finite inequalities entering the proof are verified in exact rational arithmetic, and the passage to the thermodynamic limit is proved analytically.
