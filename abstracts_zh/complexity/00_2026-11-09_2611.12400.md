# Discrete time crystals stabilized by bounded measurement noise

- **arXiv**: [2611.12400](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12400)
- **日期**: 2026-11-09
- **分类**: complexity
- **作者**: Pallabi Chatterjee, Tanay Nag, Ranjan Modak
- **NOFX相关度**: 0

## 摘要

Periodically driven clean interacting systems generically heat up, which destroys discrete time-crystalline order. Here we ask whether continuous weak measurement can stabilize such order. We study a long-range interacting spin chain whose local occupation is continuously monitored. Measurements generally induce a transition from volume-law to area-law entanglement. Yet low entanglement alone is not enough: along typical quantum trajectories, the system still reaches a featureless state with no subharmonic response. In contrast, the most-probable trajectory, obtained by suppressing measurement fluctuations, shows robust period doubling. To connect these limits, we introduce a bounded-noise protocol that restricts measurement fluctuations to a finite window. Narrow windows preserve period doubling. Intermediate windows produce coexisting time-crystalline and non-time-crystalline trajectories. Over time, trajectories leave the time-crystalline group one by one, so the order is long lived but finite. Wide windows destroy the order. Our results establish bounded measurement noise, discarding the large-deviation trajectories, as a route to time-crystalline order in clean systems. They also show that the measurement-induced area-law phase is necessary, but not sufficient, for this order.
