# Confinement-induced Transitions of Chiral Active Particles and Dumbbells

- **arXiv**: [2611.32972](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32972)
- **日期**: 2026-11-24
- **分类**: complexity
- **作者**: Mike Brandt, Falko Ziebert, Carsten Littek
- **NOFX相关度**: 0

## 摘要

We present a coherent framework for the stochastic description of a chiral Active Brownian Particle (cABP) and a chiral Active Brownian Dumbbell (cABD) in a harmonic trap. On the one hand, we employ the Martin-Siggia-Rose path integral formalism, using the passive Brownian motion as a reference state around which activity is accounted for as a perturbation series in the self-propulsion speed. This method is suited best to calculate the exact time-dependent correlation functions. On the other hand, we use the Fokker-Planck equation, which works best to obtain the steady-state probability distribution. We discuss in detail the transitions from a trap centered distribution for low activity/strong confinement to a ring-shaped distribution for high activity/low confinement and the effect of chirality, i.e. the active angular velocity. For the dumbbell, a series of such transitions occurs and the correlation functions yield information about the particle's conformation and orientation. This establishes the combined framework as a powerful tool to investigate stochastic active particle systems.
