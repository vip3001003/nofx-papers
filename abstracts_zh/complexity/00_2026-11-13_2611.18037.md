# Quantum kinetic uncertainty relations: Role of coherence in current fluctuation bounds

- **arXiv**: [2611.18037](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18037)
- **日期**: 2026-11-13
- **分类**: complexity
- **作者**: Varinder Singh, Euijoon Kwon, Jae Sung Lee
- **NOFX相关度**: 0

## 摘要

We present a comparative analysis of three quantum versions of the kinetic uncertainty relation (KUR), which incorporate coherence-dependent corrections to the classical bound. Using a three-level maser heat engine as a paradigmatic quantum thermal machine, we identify the operating regimes in which each formulation provides the tightest constraint on current fluctuations. We further identify tighter, model-specific bounds for two of these formulations, provide numerical support for both, and establish one of them analytically. Introducing ground-state degeneracy, we then demonstrate that noise-induced coherence provides a tunable resource for relocating, enhancing, or suppressing violations of the standard KUR without requiring an additional coherent drive. In particular, it enables violations in regimes where the three-level engine satisfies the classical bound. Our results clarify how distinct quantum KURs account for coherence and constrain the precision of quantum thermal machines.
