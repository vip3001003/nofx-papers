# Contact-number hyperuniformity at glass close packing in infinite dimensions

- **arXiv**: [2611.22209](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22209)
- **日期**: 2026-11-17
- **分类**: complexity
- **作者**: Harukuni Ikeda
- **NOFX相关度**: 1

## 摘要

We investigate contact-number fluctuations of harmonic spheres near glass close packing (GCP), the amorphous jamming endpoint reached by compressing an ideal glass. Using replica theory, we show that the Gardner-unstable region shrinks toward jamming as the spatial dimension increases, allowing GCP to be approached along a stable one-step replica symmetry breaking branch in the high-dimensional limit. By coupling an external field to the local contact number, we derive the variance of the total contact number and the wave-number-dependent structure factor directly from the microscopic interactions. At GCP, the structure factor vanishes quadratically at small scaled wave numbers, demonstrating contact-number hyperuniformity. Upon weak compression above GCP, the structure factor develops a low-wave-number plateau whose height is proportional to the excess coordination. These results provide an analytical description of contact-number hyperuniformity and its breakdown upon compression.
