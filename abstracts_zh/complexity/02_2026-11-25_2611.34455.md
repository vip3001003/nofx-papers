# Exact Lyapunov spectrum and localization length of the multichannel disordered Schrödinger equation

- **arXiv**: [2611.34455](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34455)
- **日期**: 2026-11-25
- **分类**: complexity
- **作者**: Aurélien Grabsch
- **NOFX相关度**: 2

## 摘要

We consider the multichannel Schrödinger equation, describing the evolution of $N$ transverse modes in a finite width wire, in the presence of a Gaussian white noise potential in the unitary symmetry class. For arbitrary energy $E$ and disorder strength $T$, we compute exactly the set of Lyapunov exponents $\{ γ_n(E,T) \}_{1 \leq n \leq N}$ which define the decay rates of the wave functions. In the large energy regime, we recover that the Lyapunov exponents are equally spaced, as predicted by the so-called DMPK approach. However we show that this property breaks down at arbitrary energy, giving rise to a richer Lyapunov spectrum. We also uncover a direct relation between the localization length, given by the inverse of the smallest Lyapunov exponent, and the density of states. Our derivation relies on the analysis of matrix stochastic differential equation for a modified Riccati matrix.
