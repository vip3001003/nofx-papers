# Predicting Quantum Mpemba Effects from Quantum Speed Limits

- **arXiv**: [2611.22547](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22547)
- **日期**: 2026-11-17
- **分类**: complexity
- **作者**: Andrea Nava, Rosario Fazio, Reinhold Egger
- **NOFX相关度**: 0

## 摘要

Can anomalous Quantum Mpemba Effects be anticipated before a system comes close to its stationary state? We employ Quantum Speed Limits as geometric indicators of Quantum Mpemba Effects, arguing that Mpemba physics is already imprinted in speed limits by benchmarking dissipative qubits and open spin chains. Our results show that short-time speed limits can predict Quantum Mpemba Effects at low cost and with reasonable precision over broad parameter regimes. This predictive power implies a useful application of speed limits for open many-body quantum systems.
