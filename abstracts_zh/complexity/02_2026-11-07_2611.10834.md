# Ising model on Calandre lattice

- **arXiv**: [2611.10834](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.10834)
- **日期**: 2026-11-07
- **分类**: complexity
- **作者**: F. A. Kassan-Ogly, A. V. Zarubin
- **NOFX相关度**: 2

## 摘要

In this paper, we proposed to consideration a noval (not yet exploited) planar lattice --- Calandre lattice with the Ising model applied to it. In the framework of an exact analytical Kramers--Wannier transfer-matrix approach and using an extension of the combinatorial Vdovichenko--Feynman method, we have obtained exact solution for Helmholtz free energy and consequent expression for the principal eigenvalue of the Kramers--Wannier transfer-matrix. In addition, we derived the exact expression for spontaneous magnetization. The usage of both expressions made it possible to perform the comprehensive studies of thermodynamic and magnetic properties of the Calandre lattice itself and a wide variety of its affiliated lattices. Among them, diversified interrelations between Calandre lattice and already known and even some novel lattices. These lattices have also revealed such effects as magnetic frustrations, multiple phase transitions and magnetic reentrancy.
