# State-space symmetry in Boolean network ensembles

- **arXiv**: [2611.19639](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.19639)
- **日期**: 2026-11-14
- **分类**: complexity
- **作者**: Yi-Kai Mo, Kevin E. Bassler
- **NOFX相关度**: 1

## 摘要

Studies of evolving Boolean network systems have found that Boolean functions cluster into equivalence classes with a characteristic frequency distribution - a pattern governed by the Zyklenzeiger symmetry group - but the dynamical origin of this symmetry has remained unexplained. Here we show that it is a consequence of a fundamental symmetry of Boolean network dynamics itself. A Boolean network consists of nodes that update according to Boolean functions of their regulatory inputs; its global dynamics is encoded in a state-space network. We identify the dynamical symmetry group of ensembles of Boolean networks that average over random interaction topologies: for networks with $N$ nodes, it is the $N$-dimensional hyperoctahedral group, generated by node-flips and node-label permutations. These transformations are admissible for every Boolean network regardless of topology; for random-topology ensembles, including all ensembles of fixed in-degree $K<N$, they constitute the complete symmetry group. We also show that input-label permutations constitute a hidden gauge freedom of the dynamics. The Zyklenzeiger symmetry of Boolean functions follows directly from the node-flips of the hyperoctahedral group together with this gauge freedom. Boolean networks related by the hyperoctahedral group have isomorphic state-space networks and form its orbits, and Burnside's lemma gives the number of these orbits for any fixed network topology. It provides a symmetry reduction of the analysis of Boolean network dynamics and explains why the Boolean functions of evolved networks fall into Zyklenzeiger classes, and why these classes split in finite networks.
