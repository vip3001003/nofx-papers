# When Time-Reversible Sensor Records Require Hidden Dissipation

- **arXiv**: [2611.24898](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24898)
- **日期**: 2026-11-18
- **分类**: complexity
- **作者**: Andrey V. Dobrynin, Anastasia Stroujkova
- **NOFX相关度**: 1

## 摘要

A binary sensor record can be time reversible yet require dissipation in every compatible finite-state Markov model. For a four-state sensor with correlated dwell times, two observed switching statistics give an exact equilibrium criterion and, when equilibrium is excluded, a positive lower bound on entropy production independent of hidden-state number. An explicit four-state construction provides an upper bound on the minimum dissipation. For a noisy Poisson sensor, the bounds differ by less than a factor of two and vanish at a kinetic matching point while the sensor remains dissipative.
