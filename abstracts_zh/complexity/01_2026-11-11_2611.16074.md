# Holographic Wilsonian RG of Yang-Mills theory in AdS

- **arXiv**: [2611.16074](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16074)
- **日期**: 2026-11-11
- **分类**: complexity
- **作者**: Soner Albayrak
- **NOFX相关度**: 1

## 摘要

In this work, we extend the equilibrium-evolution duality between stochastic quantization and Wilsonian renormalization to non-abelian gauge theories in anti-de Sitter space, for which the radial coordinate of AdS plays the role of the stochastic time. We show, to all orders in the fields for bulk theories without explicit radial dependence, that the Langevin distribution at time $t$ is the product of the ultraviolet and infrared radial wavefunctions at the cutoff $ε=t$. We therefore identify this Langevin process as the ground-state (Doob) transform of the radial evolution. By explicit calculation, we also extend the existing dictionary in the stochastic-renormalization duality: the radial component of the bulk field is Zwanziger's stochastic gauge-fixing function; radial gauge is Parisi-Wu quantization; and the bulk gauge $A_z=α^{-1}\partial_iA_i$ is Zwanziger's gauge. We also obtain the expressions for the triple-trace and quadruple-trace couplings of the holographic Wilsonian action at finite cutoff in closed form; in the infrared, they reduce to the known gluon correlators and, at the total-energy pole, to flat-space Yang-Mills amplitudes as expected. Although our explicit results are provided at tree-level and derived in AdS$_4$, we also comment on the de Sitter counterpart of these statements and on the loop corrections.
