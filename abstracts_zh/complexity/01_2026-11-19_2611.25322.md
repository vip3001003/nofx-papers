# Symmetry-resolved spectroscopy of truncation-induced perturbations in loop tensor network renormalization of the six-vertex model

- **arXiv**: [2611.25322](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25322)
- **日期**: 2026-11-19
- **分类**: complexity
- **作者**: Akira Otsuki, Yuta Yamamoto, Kokin Cho et al.
- **NOFX相关度**: 1

## 摘要

Tensor network renormalization at finite bond dimension $χ$ introduces truncation errors that are amplified by the renormalization-group (RG) flow. For loop tensor network renormalization (LoopTNR) on the $c=1$ critical line of the six-vertex model, with Luttinger parameter $K$, we identify these errors operator by operator from symmetry-resolved transfer-matrix spectra and conformal perturbation theory. With $O(2)$ symmetry imposed, the dominant error is the dimerization operator, which is allowed because LoopTNR breaks one-site translation. With less symmetry it is the $U(1)$ current or a charge-two vertex operator. The growth of the dimerization per RG step and a parameter-free ratio of its amplitudes agree with conformal perturbation theory to within a few percent. The parameter $K$ drifts slowly, owing to a weak marginal error and to the running induced by the dimerization. A larger $χ$ lowers the initial amplitude of the error for $0.75\le K\le 1.5$ but changes neither the operator nor its growth rate.
