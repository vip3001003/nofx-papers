# Detecting non-Hermitian topology from long-time dynamics

- **arXiv**: [2611.16745](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16745)
- **日期**: 2026-11-12
- **分类**: complexity
- **作者**: Antonio D'Abbruzzo, Rosario Fazio
- **NOFX相关度**: 0

## 摘要

We propose a protocol to measure the non-Hermitian topological invariant associated with the Lindbladian spectrum of chiral-symmetric open Gaussian systems from the relaxation of the covariance matrix. Importantly, the method works in the long-time regime and does not rely on isolating edge modes, so it remains applicable even when the Liouvillian skin effect may obscure them. We illustrate the technique on a Kitaev chain with reservoir-induced nonreciprocity, for which we first obtain a fully analytical and previously unknown description of its topological phase diagram. We then show that our protocol reliably distinguishes all the different phases.
