# From Fermions to Vortices: Microscopic Simulations of Phase Ordering in a d-Wave Superconductor

- **arXiv**: [2611.32916](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32916)
- **日期**: 2026-11-24
- **分类**: complexity
- **作者**: Ali Rayat, Gia-Wei Chern
- **NOFX相关度**: 1

## 摘要

Describing bath-induced superconducting phase ordering directly from a microscopic fermionic Hamiltonian remains a formidable challenge. Here we develop a microscopic framework for this problem using the recently introduced adiabatic variational principle (AVP) and demonstrate its application to a two-dimensional $d$-wave superconductor. The central construction maps evolving, spatially inhomogeneous bond-pairing fields onto constrained Bogoliubov-de Gennes states, with the associated Lagrange multipliers determining the thermodynamic driving forces. This enables dissipative evolution of the superconducting order while retaining its microscopic electronic structure, without assuming a phenomenological Ginzburg-Landau free-energy functional. Starting from disordered pairing configurations, we resolve the emergence of phase coherence through vortex-antivortex annihilation and the accompanying evolution of local electronic spectra. Vortex decay and dynamical scaling recover the characteristic kinetics of two-dimensional phase ordering, providing a nontrivial benchmark of the framework. The solver-independent AVP formulation opens a route to microscopic studies of dissipative ordering and metastability in superconductors with intertwined stripe and pair-density-wave orders.
