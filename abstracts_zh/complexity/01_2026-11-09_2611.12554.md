# Resolving the backbone of multiscale interactions through mutual information rates

- **arXiv**: [2611.12554](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12554)
- **日期**: 2026-11-09
- **分类**: complexity
- **作者**: Alberto Raiola, Giorgio Nicoletti, Daniel Maria Busiello
- **NOFX相关度**: 1

## 摘要

Natural systems are often composed of processes evolving on multiple timescales and coupled through complex interaction networks. Inferring such networks is especially challenging when timescales are widely separated, since resolving the fastest processes while accurately sampling the slowest ones is generally prohibitive. In this work, we propose a framework to reconstruct a multiscale interaction tree by combining a rescaled version of the mutual information rate, its conditional counterpart, and the ordering of timescales. We derive an exact theory for stationary Gaussian processes, finding that these measures identify directly connected processes, with the asymptotic mutual information rate distinguishing fast-to-slow from slow-to-fast couplings. We show that this reconstruction can be achieved from the spectral matrix and illustrate its robustness to finite trajectory lengths, moderate timescale separation, and weak nonlinearities. Our work paves the way for exploiting the presence of multiple timescales in real-world systems as a source of structural identifiability rather than an obstacle to network inference.
