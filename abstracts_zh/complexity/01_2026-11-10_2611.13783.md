# Critical geometry of triconnected clusters in percolation

- **arXiv**: [2611.13783](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13783)
- **日期**: 2026-11-10
- **分类**: complexity
- **作者**: Qi Wang, Youjin Deng, Ming Li
- **NOFX相关度**: 1

## 摘要

We investigate the critical geometry of triconnected clusters in percolation, which remain connected after the removal of any two sites. By combining biconnected-component decomposition with the SPQR decomposition of graphs, we develop an efficient algorithm for identifying triconnected clusters in large systems. Extensive Monte Carlo simulations yield estimates of the fractal dimension $d_3=1.2308(3)$, $1.083(2)$, and $0.77(2)$ in dimensions $d=2$, $3$, and $4$, respectively, revealing a systematic decrease of $d_3$ with increasing $d$. In dimension $d=6$, the largest triconnected cluster grows logarithmically with system size, whereas on complete graphs, corresponding to $d=\infty$, it approaches a finite size. Both behaviors indicate a vanishing fractal dimension, $d_3=0$, for $d\geq6$. Furthermore, the singular contribution to the susceptibility-like quantity associated with the second moment of the triconnected-cluster size distribution scales as $χ\sim L^{2d_3-d}$. Consequently, it vanishes for $d\geq3$, leaving a finite asymptotic susceptibility, in contrast to the divergent behavior observed in standard percolation.
