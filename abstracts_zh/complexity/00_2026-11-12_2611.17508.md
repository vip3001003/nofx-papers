# In-plane diffusion, spurious drift and a physics-structured gap predictor for a free colloidal pair

- **arXiv**: [2611.17508](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17508)
- **日期**: 2026-11-12
- **分类**: complexity
- **作者**: Juan Pablo Staforelli Vivanco, Cristobal Augusto Valdés Cruces
- **NOFX相关度**: 0

## 摘要

When the diffusivity of a Brownian particle depends on position, its mean displacement acquires a noise-induced (spurious) drift whose magnitude is set by the stochastic convention $α$. We ask how much of this physics can be extracted from a pair of 1.95 $μ$m polystyrene spheres released from blinking holographic optical tweezers at five bath temperatures between 2 and 40 $^\circ$C. The single-sphere diffusivity inside the pair stays between 0.093 and 0.098 $μ$m$^2$ s$^{-1}$, whereas the Stokes-Einstein value for water rises from 0.126 to 0.361 $μ$m$^2$ s$^{-1}$; an isolated sphere reproduces the deficit, which is therefore not a pair effect. From the measured gap-dependent diffusivities $D_\parallel(h)$ and $D_\perp(h)$ we build a Fokker-Planck predictor of the future gap and score it on held-out releases. It recovers the 90% width of the gap distribution up to 800 ms but cannot discriminate between $α= 0$, $1/2$ and $1$, because the $α$-dependent mean shift is an order of magnitude smaller than that width. The ensemble spurious drift is consistent with zero at both ends of the temperature range, and the regression for $α$ never exceeds $R^2 = 0.22$. We therefore present a planar methods baseline and identify the measurements required to resolve $α$.
