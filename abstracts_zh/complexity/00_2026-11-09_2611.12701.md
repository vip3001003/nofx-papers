# One-Particle Resonances and Eigenstate Thermalization in Periodic Free-Fermion Lattices

- **arXiv**: [2611.12701](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12701)
- **日期**: 2026-11-09
- **分类**: complexity
- **作者**: Tristan Benoist, Vojkan Jakšić, Claude-Alain Pillet et al.
- **NOFX相关度**: 0

## 摘要

We study eigenstate fluctuations of the particle number in the nearest-neighbour free-fermion model on the periodic cubic lattice $(\mathbb{Z}/L\mathbb{Z})^d$. For every fixed dimension, every particle number, every subvolume, and every normalized energy eigenstate, we prove a bound of order $NL^{d-1}$ for the particle-number variance. The corresponding eigenstate-thermalization estimate is of order $L^{d-1}/(Nη^2)$ for fluctuations of relative size $η$. In particular, at positive particle density the bound decays as the inverse linear size. The proof decomposes the centered particle-number operator according to one-particle energy transfer and treats the zero- and nonzero-transfer components separately. The nonzero-transfer part is controlled by a sharp resonance bound, proved from Mann's theorem on relations among roots of unity, while the zero-transfer part is estimated by a Hilbert-Schmidt argument. These bounds are optimal in every dimension: the maximal nonzero-gap multiplicity has order $L^{d-1}$, and suitable many-body eigenstates realize matching variance lower bounds and show that the concentration bound cannot be improved in estimates uniform in the tolerance parameter.
