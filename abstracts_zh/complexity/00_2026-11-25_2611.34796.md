# Discotic columnar phase in a three-dimensional monomer-tetramer model of Heilmann and Lieb with side-to-side attraction

- **arXiv**: [2611.34796](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34796)
- **日期**: 2026-11-25
- **分类**: complexity
- **作者**: Qidong He
- **NOFX相关度**: 0

## 摘要

We study Model III of Heilmann--Lieb (1979), a lattice model for liquid crystals consisting of tetramers (plaquettes) on the cubic lattice at chemical potential $μ$, interacting via a hard-core repulsion and a lateral attraction of strength $a>0$ between coplanar, aligned tetramers. The model was conjectured to exhibit liquid-crystalline order at low temperature provided that $μ+2a>0$. We establish discotic columnar order throughout this regime, fully resolving the conjecture. Specifically, we prove the existence of exactly twelve extremal and periodic Gibbs measures, each breaking rotational symmetry, with one of three tetramer orientations dominant, and translational symmetry in exactly two directions, with one of four tetramer parities dominant within that orientation. Thus, the tetramers form one-dimensional columns arranged on a square lattice. This arrangement is not a priori evident from the zero-temperature picture, where energy only enforces a layered structure: each layer is fully packed and crystalline, but different layers are free to shift independently within their planes. The main novelty here lies in showing how thermal fluctuations lift this degeneracy in favor of the twelve ground states in which all tetramers share a common parity.
