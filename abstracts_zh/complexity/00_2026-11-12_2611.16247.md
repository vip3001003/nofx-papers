# Quantum Cost of Emptiness in a One-Dimensional Bose Gas

- **日期**: 2026-11-12
- **分类**: complexity
- **作者**: Bin Huang, Ziyan Zhu

## 摘要

The probability $P_0(R)$ of an empty interval of radius $R$ probes the far tail of density fluctuations in the thermodynamic ground state of the repulsive Lieb--Liniger gas. Here $ρ$ is the bulk density and $c$ the repulsion. As $\ell = ρR \to \infty$ at fixed $γ= c/ρ> 0$, we find $-\log P_0 = f(γ)\ell^2 + B(γ)\sqrt{\ell} - \frac{1}{4}\log\ell + O_γ(1)$, with no linear term and $B = 2ζ(3/2)\sqrt{f/(πγ)}$. Exact Gaussian completion of the auxiliary-field determinant reduces the leading collective response to an electrostatic slit problem, yielding $f(γ)$ in elliptic integrals. Its dipole polarization tail drives auxiliary scattering returns whose local density saturates while the active rapidity range expands as $\sqrt{R}$, producing the square-root boundary cost. The full return spectrum and the occupied-rapidity endpoints together fix the $-1/4$ logarithmic coefficient. Through the contact identity, widening the void probes a dense healing scale at the boundary whose growing characteristic occupation supports an emergent classical-field description even in a strongly interacting bulk.
