# Continuous-Time Random Walks with Internal Stochastic Renewal Process

- **arXiv**: [2611.16383](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16383)
- **日期**: 2026-11-12
- **分类**: complexity
- **作者**: Boris Z. Huang
- **NOFX相关度**: 1

## 摘要

We introduce a continuous-time random walk process via a structured density by tracking the state of the particle as the system evolves. Whenever the particle makes a jump, its internal state either increases by one or resets to zero, so that the internal state evolves according to a renewal process. A wide range of governing integro-differential equations may be obtained from this formulation, some of which exhibit various types of anomalous behaviour. For certain choices of reset probabilities, waiting times and jump distributions, we explicitly derive the mode-dependent memory kernels and investigate their continuum limits. The framework can recover the time-and-space-fractional Fokker-Planck equation, which traditionally models the interplay between long wait times and large jumps and exhibits anomalous diffusion. Furthermore, we obtain a process which converges toward the equilibrium distribution as time approaches some finite value through a sequence of waiting times which decrease geometrically. Finally, we investigate a process that incorporates self-excitation and self-inhibition into the waiting times for each state to produce a mean square displacement proportional to $t^ν$ for any $ν> 0$. Through these examples, we illustrate the link between the microscopic mechanisms in these processes and their non-local macroscopic governing equations.
