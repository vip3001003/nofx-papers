# Surface codes with coherent errors: optimal decoding and finite-size crossovers

- **arXiv**: [2611.13098](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13098)
- **日期**: 2026-11-09
- **分类**: complexity
- **作者**: Xu-Dong Dai, Zhou-Quan Wan, Xi Dai et al.
- **NOFX相关度**: 0

## 摘要

We study the surface code under uniform coherent $Z$ rotations $\exp{(iθZ)}$ followed by perfect syndrome measurements, a setting known to exhibit strong finite-size effects that complicate the extraction of the asymptotic decoding threshold. We identify finite-size behavior of both Pauli decoding measures and the coherent information with two characteristic lengths: an algebraic ballistic-crossover scale $d_{\mathrm{ba}}\sim 1/β_R^α$ ($α=1$ for odd $d$ and $α=2$ for even $d$) and a much larger renormalization-group scale $d_\mathrm{RG}\sim \exp{(C/β^{4}_R)}$, where $β_R=-\tfrac{1}{2}\ln\tanθ$ and $d$ is the system size. As $θ$ approaches the ballistic SWAP point $π/4$, the two length scales diverge at very different rates, leaving a broad intermediate regime $d_{\mathrm{ba}}\ll d\ll d_\mathrm{RG}$ in which these quantities show substantial finite-size drift and approximate plateaus. To establish the microscopic basis of this crossover picture, we derive a leading inter-flavor quartic interaction of order $β_R^2$ in the replicated Majorana theory, which provides a route to the class-D nonlinear sigma model. The resulting marginal RG flow motivates the scaling variable $X_4=β_R^4\ln d$, in which free-fermion simulations show approximate collapse of decoding measures. These results are consistent with a Pauli-decoding threshold at $θ_c=π/4$ in the thermodynamic limit. For any even $d$, we prove that maximum-likelihood Pauli decoding maximizes entanglement fidelity over all deterministic syndrome-conditioned quantum recoveries, and that its decoding threshold attains the quantum-optimal limit, coinciding with the one defined by coherent information.
