# Giant Diffusion at Flocking Transitions

- **arXiv**: [2611.12465](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12465)
- **日期**: 2026-11-09
- **分类**: complexity
- **作者**: Sohini Chatterjee, Wasim Akram, Subir K. Das
- **NOFX相关度**: 2

## 摘要

We simulate dynamics in two-dimensional aligning active-matter models exhibiting order-disorder transitions in the velocity field. Analysis reveals that the single-particle diffusivity, $D_s$, exhibits strong enhancements at criticality which we quantify via a finite-size scaling method. This dynamical behavior at nonequilibrium flocking transitions is fundamentally different from that at the criticality of equilibrium systems, where diffusivities either vanish or remain non-critical. Furthermore, $D_s$ exhibits pronounced finite-size effects throughout the ordered phase, which also are absent in equilibrium settings. We interpret these counterintuitive transport anomalies within the framework of a flocking theory. Our results suggest that enhanced diffusion generates effective long-range interactions. This relaxes the constraints of the Mermin-Wagner theorem that otherwise prevents order-disorder transitions in two dimensions for systems possessing continuous symmetry. These results are of much experimental relevance and have important implications in formulating quantitative theories for nonequilibrium transports.
