# Optimal heterogeneous diffusivity profiles for mean first-passage minimization

- **arXiv**: [2611.17470](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17470)
- **日期**: 2026-11-12
- **分类**: complexity
- **作者**: Luiz Menon
- **NOFX相关度**: 0

## 摘要

We investigate how spatially varying diffusivity can be optimized to minimize the mean first-passage time for escape from a spherical shell with an inner reflecting boundary and an outer absorbing boundary. Under a fixed volume-averaged diffusivity, we derive a variational condition for arbitrary spatial dimension and stochastic integration prescription. For particles starting at the inner boundary, we obtain exact optimal profiles in one dimension, with homogeneous diffusion being optimal under the Stratonovich prescription. In higher dimensions, the optimal Stratonovich profile becomes decreasing. The optimal Itô profile is decreasing in all dimensions, whereas the optimal Hänggi--Klimontovich profile is increasing in one and two dimensions and can exhibit a maximum in dimensions greater than two. For particles starting beyond the inner boundary, optimal profiles can exhibit a discontinuity at the initial position, with constant diffusivity in the inner region. Numerical optimization complements these results, showing how geometry, stochastic prescription, and initial position jointly determine the diffusivity distribution that minimizes the mean escape time.
