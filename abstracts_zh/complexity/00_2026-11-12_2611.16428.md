# Why "quantum spin liquids'' are quantum singlet liquids

- **arXiv**: [2611.16428](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16428)
- **日期**: 2026-11-12
- **分类**: complexity
- **作者**: Sergey Syzranov, Arthur P. Ramirez
- **NOFX相关度**: 0

## 摘要

The last couple of decades have seen enormous theoretical and experimental research efforts aimed at finding quantum spin liquids (QSLs) in geometrically frustrated (GF) magnetic materials. Experimental demonstrations of QSLs are challenging due to the indirect character of possible QSL signatures and the presence of impurities in materials, which may destroy or obscure a QSL. At the same time, theoretical literature often lacks consensus about the ground states of quantum spins on even the simplest GF magnetic lattices. We summarize experimental evidence that in the impurity-free limit, available GF magnetic materials that do not develop magnetic order at low temperatures would form {\it a quantum singlet liquid}, i.e. a disordered state of non-magnetic excitations. We further confirm the existence of such a singlet liquid and its gapless character with theoretical arguments. Such a liquid manifests itself in the thermodynamics of GF magnets while having no magnetic features at low temperatures.
