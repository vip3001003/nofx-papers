# Quantum entanglement as a resource for self-organization and stabilization

- **arXiv**: [2611.11175](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.11175)
- **日期**: 2026-11-08
- **分类**: complexity
- **作者**: Kazuki Ikeda
- **NOFX相关度**: 1

## 摘要

Can quantum entanglement generate macroscopic order that persists after the entanglement itself disappears? We establish such a mechanism in a driven-dissipative bosonic lattice, where local entanglement enables transport that amplifies spatial fluctuations into extended Turing patterns. For the full nonlinear dynamics with noise and disorder, we derive rigorous finite-time bounds on pattern intensity for all admissible separable evolutions, allowing arbitrary classical correlations. Quantum dynamics produces pattern intensities beyond these bounds during both formation and stabilization under noise. The excess intensity also provides a lower bound on the accumulated transport contribution beyond the separable limit. We further introduce an environment that responds to emerging order by suppressing local coherence and activating classical transport. The patterns then persist as local entanglement is lost, with both stages arising from a single time-independent Lindblad equation. These results show how transient quantum resources can establish persistent macroscopic order, opening new directions for harnessing self-organization in nonequilibrium systems.
