# Data-enhanced self-learning projective quantum Monte Carlo with Rydberg-atom measurements

- **arXiv**: [2611.34636](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34636)
- **日期**: 2026-11-25
- **分类**: complexity
- **作者**: Vichayuth Imchitr, Luca Brodoloni, Sebastiano Pilati et al.
- **NOFX相关度**: 3

## 摘要

We introduce a data-enhanced self-learning projective quantum Monte Carlo (PQMC) method that incorporates projective measurements from programmable quantum simulators into the guiding wavefunction of a ground-state projection. We demonstrate the approach for a two-dimensional Rydberg-atom array using restricted Boltzmann machine (RBM) and recurrent neural network (RNN) guiding wavefunctions. Experimental pretraining reduces finite-population effects and accelerates self-learning convergence, allowing unbiased estimation of ground-state properties to be obtained with fewer walkers and fewer self-learning iterations. The improvement is particularly pronounced for the RBM, while the more accurate RNN guiding wavefunction exhibits a smaller, though still observable, benefit from pretraining. Simulations across different system sizes suggest that the relative advantage of experimental pretraining may increase with system size. Improvements are also observed for the checkerboard order parameter, despite deviations of the experimental measurements from the equilibrium numerical results. Finally, finite-size scaling with self-learning PQMC without experimental pretraining yields the critical point and critical exponents of the disordered-to-checkerboard transition in agreement with previous numerical studies. Our results show that limited and imperfect experimental measurements can provide an effective initialization for self-learning PQMC, while imaginary-time projection combined with subsequent unsupervised learning progressively refines the guiding wavefunction toward the ground state.
