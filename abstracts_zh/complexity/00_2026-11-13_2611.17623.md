# Magic of Dicke states: density, cost and yield

- **arXiv**: [2611.17623](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17623)
- **日期**: 2026-11-13
- **分类**: complexity
- **作者**: Yves H. Kwan
- **NOFX相关度**: 0

## 摘要

Dicke states are an elementary family of many-body quantum states, consisting of equal superpositions of all configurations of $L$ qubits with $n$ excitations. We show that their magic, as measured by the stabilizer Rényi entropy (SRE), is extensive at every filling $f=n/L$ except half-filling, and we obtain the magic density analytically, together with the subleading terms. In the context of fault-tolerant quantum computation, we also investigate the yield of Dicke states, and find that they can produce on average an extensive number of CCZ states (or $T$ states with a catalyst) at any $0<f<1$. This yield is not self-averaging, but fluctuates from run to run by $O(L)$, as if it were set by a hidden phase shared by all qubits. At half-filling, the SRE grows only logarithmically, while the preparation cost, average yield and relative entropy of magic remain extensive, so that a small SRE does not necessarily signal a weak resource. These results can be understood by viewing the Dicke state as a fixed-magnetization projection of a product of tilted spins.
