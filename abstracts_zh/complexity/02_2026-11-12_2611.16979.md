# Cup and cap synchronization: higher-order node dynamics from topology

- **arXiv**: [2611.16979](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16979)
- **日期**: 2026-11-12
- **分类**: complexity
- **作者**: Runyue Wang, Yuanzhao Zhang, Ginestra Bianconi
- **NOFX相关度**: 2

## 摘要

Higher-order node-based synchronization provides distinct mechanisms for discontinuous synchronization transitions. However, a comprehensive theoretical framework capable of encompassing existing models and revealing the role of higher-order interactions beyond triangles is still missing. Here, combining algebraic topology and nonlinear dynamics, we introduce Cup and Cap Synchronization, a unifying theoretical framework for higher-order node-based dynamics that naturally incorporates the topological organization of the underlying higher-order network. We show that Cup and Cap Synchronization can display critical behavior belonging to the universality classes of two existing higher-order Kuramoto-like models, whose distinct critical behaviors were thought to originate from different mechanisms. Remarkably, we demonstrate that Cup and Cap Synchronization displays additional phenomenology not captured by the previously proposed models of higher-order node-based synchronization. Most relevantly, the inclusion of four-body interactions can fundamentally alter the critical properties of the synchronization transition, giving rise to collective phenomena that cannot be observed in models including only interactions mediated by edges and triangles. These findings reveal the importance of going beyond the commonly studied three-body terms and establish Cup and Cap Synchronization as a unifying theoretical framework for understanding collective dynamics in higher-order networks.
