# Deriving Tsallis Entropy from First Principles

- **arXiv**: [2611.12441](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12441)
- **日期**: 2026-11-09
- **分类**: complexity
- **作者**: J. A. S. Lima, M. H. Benetti
- **NOFX相关度**: 0

## 摘要

The kinetic approach for q-distribution is extended through a "bottom-up" procedure to the corresponding entropic formula. In this way, the validity of Tsallis' formula, S_q(W) = k_B ln_q(W), is established from first principles. The resulting q-entropy is additive and the zeroth law of thermodynamics is automatically satisfied because the conventional identification beta = 1/(k_B T) remains valid. Entropy, energy, and all relevant quantities for both discrete and continuous cases are evaluated using standard (linear) averages. The "bottom-up" and "top-down" approaches yield the same q-distribution for velocities. The standard results are naturally recovered in the limit q -> 1.
