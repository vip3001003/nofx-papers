# Persistent Hydration and Ionic Mobility: When the Ion Is Not the Only Transported Species

- **arXiv**: [2611.23605](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23605)
- **日期**: 2026-11-17
- **分类**: complexity
- **作者**: Biman Bagchi
- **NOFX相关度**: 0

## 摘要

Small-ion transport in water remains only partially understood despite more than a century of theoretical work. The difficulty is especially acute for strongly hydrated ions such as Li$^{+}$, Mg$^{2+}$, and Ca$^{2+}$, whose first hydration shells can persist on timescales comparable to or longer than translational motion. We develop a molecular framework in which the ion, its first hydration shell, and the outer solvent are treated as dynamically coupled components. Local hydration structure and exchange are represented through a potential of mean force and coordination dynamics, while collective solvent response is described through molecular memory functions. The theory clarifies why hydration cannot generally be represented by an additive friction or a single enlarged radius, and provides a route beyond conventional continuum and molecular descriptions.
