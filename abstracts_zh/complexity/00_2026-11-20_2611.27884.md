# Thermo-electric Response Bound on Fluctuations in Coherent Transport

- **arXiv**: [2611.27884](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27884)
- **日期**: 2026-11-20
- **分类**: complexity
- **作者**: Gianmichele Blasi, Rosa López, Mykhailo Moskalets et al.
- **NOFX相关度**: 0

## 摘要

We establish an accessible lower bound on current noise in coherent electron transport, expressed in terms of responses to reservoir temperatures and chemical potentials. Derived within the Floquet-Nambu scattering formalism using a Cramér-Rao-type inequality, it holds for arbitrary linear combinations of charge and heat currents in normal terminals, including those of hybrid superconducting systems, under static or periodic driving. The bound is tight in experimentally relevant regimes, notably in quantum point contacts with static or oscillating voltages and normal-superconductor junctions, often providing stronger constraints than thermodynamic uncertainty relations. It can be evaluated via experimentally accessible response coefficients, without inferring entropy production or dynamical activity, and provides useful constraints on the precision of nanoscale thermal and quantum devices.
