# Geometric Theory of Quasiperiodically Driven Systems

- **arXiv**: [2611.16683](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16683)
- **日期**: 2026-11-12
- **分类**: complexity
- **作者**: Chengzhi Ye, Marin Bukov
- **NOFX相关度**: 2

## 摘要

Quasiperiodically driven systems give rise to a rich set of physical phenomena in contemporary quantum physics, with applications ranging from nonequilibrium dynamics to Hamiltonian engineering and control. However, unlike periodically driven (Floquet) systems, their study is hindered by the absence of a generic theory to describe their evolution using an effective time-independent Hamiltonian and its eigenstates. Based on counterdiabatic driving, we construct a general framework to identify and determine the dynamically invariant spaces of arbitrary quasiperiodic drives (i.e., the generalization of Floquet states). We decompose their unitary evolution using the exact effective average-energy operator Æ and the corresponding gauge potential, which we compute via a variational principle in frequency-lattice operator space. We then formulate a four-fold taxonomy of quasiperiodic drives based on the geometry and topology of their dynamically invariant spaces into (non)abelian and (un)twisted classes. The abelian untwisted class admits a direct extension of Floquet's theorem to incommensurate quasiperiodic drives; going beyond, we derive a nonabelian generalization of Floquet theory. Finally, we propose a concept of ergodicity whose onset is marked by coalescing dynamically invariant eigenspaces, in direct analogy to the emergence of ergodicity in a mixed classical phase space. The effective mathematical framework, variational principle, and nonabelian ergodicity we put forward apply beyond quasiperiodic drives.
