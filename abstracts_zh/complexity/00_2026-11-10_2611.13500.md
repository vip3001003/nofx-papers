# Facilitated Relaxation Overrides Energetic Pathways in a Multiphase Square-Kagome Artificial Spin Ice

- **arXiv**: [2611.13500](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13500)
- **日期**: 2026-11-10
- **分类**: complexity
- **作者**: Wen-Cheng Yue, Zixiong Yuan, Peiyuan Huang et al.
- **NOFX相关度**: 0

## 摘要

Relaxation in interacting many-body systems is typically assumed to follow energy minimization, yet local dynamical rules can determine which microscopic pathways are accessible. In facilitated relaxation, these pathways are enabled or blocked by neighboring configurations. However, isolating such rules in natural materials is difficult because energetics, disorder and dynamics are usually entangled. Here we realize a geometrically tunable square-kagome artificial spin ice-a lithographic nanomagnet array-that provides a clean, designable platform for facilitated relaxation. The array hosts two tunable nearest-neighbor interactions and supports multiple low-energy phases: spin crystal, spin liquid and spin foam. In the spin-foam phase, abundant facilitator configurations allow relaxation to follow Boltzmann statistics. In the spin-crystal phase, by contrast, domain-wall excitations are trapped in non-Boltzmann distributions, although energetically preferred relaxation channels exist. The ordered crystal background lacks the facilitators needed to connect topologically distinct domain-wall classes. These classes are therefore kinetically isolated, as observed in both experiment and Monte Carlo simulation. Our results show that transition-network topology can override energetic relaxation, establishing artificial spin ice as a programmable platform for constrained dynamics and nonergodic relaxation.
