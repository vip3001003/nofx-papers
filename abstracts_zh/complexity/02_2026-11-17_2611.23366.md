# Exact Solutions to Matrix Models and String Theories: The Global Construction

- **arXiv**: [2611.23366](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23366)
- **日期**: 2026-11-17
- **分类**: complexity
- **作者**: Jasper Kager, João Rodrigues, Ricardo Schiappa et al.
- **NOFX相关度**: 2

## 摘要

Exact nonperturbative solutions to matrix models and string theories are obtained in closed-form via a discrete-Fourier/Zak-transform representation of their partition-function resurgent-transseries. Examples include all hermitian one-matrix models (paired with their topological string duals) alongside all their multicritical and minimal-string double-scaling limits. The resulting structure is resonant, necessarily including both eigenvalues (D-branes) and anti-eigenvalues (negative-tension-branes). In a complementary preceding paper these solutions were extensively verified at the local level. In order to study them globally across their phase diagrams, complete non-linear Stokes data (and corresponding connection formulae) are still needed -- all of which are obtained herein. This involves a careful study of (anti-) eigenvalue tunneling across the complete multi-sheeted structure of their associated spectral-curves, and how the result descends from multi-cut smooth-curves to single-cut singular-curves -- all of which are extensively checked via large-order analysis and matrix-integral computations. Making use of the resulting exact Stokes automorphisms, nonperturbative solutions can be fully tested, from weak-to-strong 't Hooft coupling, as well as from large-to-small N, and for any complex-values of all parameters, by correctly implementing Stokes-transitions at the crossing of all possible Stokes-lines. Several analytical and numerical tests are carried through in diverse examples covering from matrix models through non-critical strings, which produce very sharp numerical matches fully validating our exact analytical expressions. Anti-eigenvalues or negative-tension D-branes are an absolutely essential part of the story in order to find such precise agreements.
