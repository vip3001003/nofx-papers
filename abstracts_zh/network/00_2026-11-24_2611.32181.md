# Multi-Scale Temporal Structure of the Conti Ransomware Communication Network

- **arXiv**: [2611.32181](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32181)
- **日期**: 2026-11-24
- **分类**: network
- **作者**: Cameron Cornell, Matthew Roughan, Lewis Mitchell et al.
- **NOFX相关度**: 0

## 摘要

Communication networks provide a means of inferring broader organisational structure from accumulated local interactions. Cybercriminal organisations are particularly vulnerable to such inference, despite their secretive nature, because their distributed operation necessitates coordination through centralised digital communication infrastructure. The public leak of the Conti ransomware organisation's internal communications provides a rare longitudinal record of this coordination. Yet common approaches to analysing such longitudinal records aggregate interactions into a static network or model the communication process as stationary, both assuming a temporal homogeneity rarely tested for criminal organisations. Here we show substantial temporal heterogeneity at each scale examined in the Conti record, and demonstrate that much of it can be accounted for through appropriate, diagnostically guided choices of event representation and observation period. Collapsing rapid message bursts into conversational episodes brings communication timing closer to a constant-intensity reference and clarifies longer-timescale structure, which resolves into a sequence of distinct communication regimes, including a major transition following a coordinated platform migration. Even so, within these regimes heterogeneity persists, most evident in churn among peripheral participants, while highly active actors remain consistently observable. Together, these findings motivate explicit consideration of multi-scale temporal dynamics in the analysis, modelling and longitudinal monitoring of cybercriminal networks.
