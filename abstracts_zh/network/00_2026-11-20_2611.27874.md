# Network versus market information in coupled opinion-return dynamics

- **arXiv**: [2611.27874](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27874)
- **日期**: 2026-11-20
- **分类**: network
- **作者**: Yue Yu, Luis Enrique Correa Rocha
- **NOFX相关度**: 0

## 摘要

When making investment decisions, investors can use common signals from the market and local signals from their social networks. We develop an agent-based opinion-return model in which agents follow fixed trading strategies and respond to either local network information or a common market signal. In our model, trend followers and contrarians select either a local network signal or a common market signal and then update their buy, hold, or sell opinions by following or opposing the signal, respectively. The aggregate opinion imbalance determines the return, which sets the market signal. We find that three collective regimes emerge depending on how much network information is used and the fraction of contrarians in the population. Persistent directional states occur when trend followers dominate, market-wide reversals appear when a contrarian-rich population relies on the common market signal, and opposing opinions coexist with small aggregate returns when the same population relies on network signals. With a common market signal, the majority strategy has the higher payoff scores, whereas increasing reliance on network signals reduces this difference. When only network information is used, higher network clustering is associated with greater polarisation and smaller return magnitudes when trend followers dominate. Degree heterogeneity has little effect on aggregate measures but changes the payoff scores of high- and low-degree agents. In contrarian-rich populations, local information thus reduces aggregate return fluctuations even as opposing opinions persist.
