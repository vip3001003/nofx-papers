# Locating Debunkers for Rumor Mitigation in Directed Networks

- **arXiv**: [2611.14825](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14825)
- **日期**: 2026-11-10
- **分类**: network
- **作者**: Kasra Ghorbani, Xinshan Jiao, Tao Zhou
- **NOFX相关度**: 1

## 摘要

Rumors can spread widely through social networks, making the selection of a limited number of debunkers to disseminate corrective information important. Existing centrality-based methods only consider the individual influence of a node on spreading processes. Those individually influential nodes often have heavily overlapping influence ranges, resulting in a limited number of users they can cover. To address this problem, we propose the so-called Hybrid Marginal Influence (HMI) framework, which combines node centrality with additional audience coverage provided by each candidate and adaptively balances these two factors through a greedy selection procedure. We further develop the Debunker-Augmented SIR (DA-SIR) model for the propagation of rumors and corrective information. Experiments on ten real-world directed networks show that HMI generally selects debunkers with broader audience coverage than conventional centrality-based methods, accompanied by a lower final cumulative fraction of users who repost the rumor.
