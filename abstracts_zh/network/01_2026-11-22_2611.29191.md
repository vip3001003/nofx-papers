# Physical co-presence of workers is linked with the evolution of interfirm networks

- **arXiv**: [2611.29191](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.29191)
- **日期**: 2026-11-22
- **分类**: network
- **作者**: Ketian Chen, Hiroyasu Inoue, Yoshihide Sekimoto et al.
- **NOFX相关度**: 1

## 摘要

Inter-firm transaction networks underpin modern economies and are critical for economic growth and resilience. Previous studies have largely explained the formation of inter-firm ties through spatial proximity. Yet people routinely move across space, creating opportunities for encounters that extend beyond geographical proximity. How these opportunities relate to inter-firm transaction relationships remains poorly understood. Here, we combine large-scale mobile phone mobility data with a multi-year panel of transactions among 196,317 firms in Tokyo to quantify encounter opportunities between workers and examine their association with inter-firm transaction ties. We find that pairs of areas with greater opportunities for encounters one another are more likely to have transaction links and new link formation between previously unconnected firms. This association is particularly pronounced in information- and service-intensive industries, reflecting the importance of encounter opportunities for exchange involving intangible knowledge and service. Indirect connections through shared neighbors in the encounter network are also positively associated with subsequent transaction formation across industries, but not within industries. These findings suggest that urban policies shaping connectivity formed through human mobility could play a significant role in the development and resilience of economic networks.
