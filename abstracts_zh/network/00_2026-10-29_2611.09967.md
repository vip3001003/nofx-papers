# Persistent A-homotopy of Complex Networks

- **arXiv**: [2611.09967](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09967)
- **日期**: 2026-10-29
- **分类**: network
- **作者**: Milan Rajkovic
- **NOFX相关度**: 0

## 摘要

We study how loop classes in a network change when cycles up to a prescribed length are declared contractible. The resulting sequence of quotient groups contains the fundamental group of the clique complex at length three and the graph $A$-fundamental group at length four. Its mod-two abelianisation has a barcode whose death scales are the lengths in a minimum cycle basis. This separates the choice of a network filtration from the choice of local loop relations and provides a computable connection between persistent $A$-homotopy and persistent homology. We illustrate the construction on lattices, Watts--Strogatz networks and nine benchmark networks. Filling the plaquettes of a periodic square lattice recovers the fundamental group of the torus. In the Watts--Strogatz simulations, the normalised largest death scale falls below the normalised mean path length over the sampled range, while most clique-homology classes survive the square relations at small rewiring probabilities. The power grid has fewer surviving classes but a longer tail of death scales than five degree-preserving randomisations. We also examine connectivity graphs of maximal cliques in ego networks. Explicit certificates establish non-trivial $A$-classes even though the original ego clique complexes are contractible. The group construction and its linear summary answer related but distinct questions about which loop relations a network model permit.
