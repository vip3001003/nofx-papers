# Empirical Limits of Risk-Averse Reinforcement Learning versus Gas-Aware Routing in Deterministic DeFi Execution

- **arXiv**: [2611.27987](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27987)
- **日期**: 2026-05-18
- **分类**: ml
- **作者**: Sri Sairam Gautam B
- **NOFX相关度**: 4

## 摘要

The migration of order execution to Automated Market Makers (AMMs) introduces two deterministic frictions absent from classical microstructure: closed-form constant-product slippage and Maximal Extractable Value (MEV) sandwich attacks gated by gas costs. We construct an end-to-end execution environment calibrated to live Uniswap V2 reserves, an EIP-1559 AR(1) gas process, and real Swap events, utilising stress-volatility gas shocks to make the MEV trigger informative. A Proximal Policy Optimisation (PPO) agent with a Rockafellar-Uryasev Conditional Value-at-Risk (CVaR) penalty and a dual-head Chow-Ghavamzadeh critic is trained for one million steps across five risk-aversion levels and seeds, benchmarked against a myopic gas-aware oracle. We document a reproducible specification-gaming pathology: prior to a patch, a forced terminal liquidation routed through a no-MEV code path artificially inflated returns threefold and was preferentially exploited by higher-$λ$ runs. On the corrected environment, the strongest PPO variant captures 79\% of the myopic gas-aware oracle (46,542 $\pm$ 673 vs 58,786 $\pm$ 616 USDC), and extending the observation with three blocks of gas history fails to close the gap. We find no empirical evidence that the tested model-free MLP policies dominate this myopic gas-aware oracle, which can be evaluated in $\mathcal{O}(\log ε^{-1})$ time per block. This result is robust to a $\sim$16x trunk-capacity expansion and a variance-controlled gas-history ablation. Code and reproducibility materials are available upon request.
