# Optimal execution in a fractional setting with reinforcement learning

- **arXiv**: [2611.11373](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.11373)
- **日期**: 2026-11-08
- **分类**: ml
- **作者**: Matthieu Garcin, Jiang Pu, Thomas Verrier
- **NOFX相关度**: 2

## 摘要

We consider the problem of optimal liquidation with linear market impact, as in the seminal work of Almgren and Chriss, but with an asset driven by a fractional Brownian motion. For $H \neq 1/2$, we observe that the deterministic solution given by Almgren and Chriss is no longer optimal. We develop a strategy to exploit the information induced by the correlation between price increments. The strategy is derived from a policy gradient algorithm, parameterized by a neural network, trained to maximise the CARA utility, for which our method allows for significant improvements.
