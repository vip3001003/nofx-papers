# The Double Entropy Fit Index (DEFI): An Information-Theoretic Approach to Dimensionality Reduction and Clustering

- **arXiv**: [2611.23523](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23523)
- **日期**: 2026-11-17
- **分类**: info_theory
- **作者**: Gabriel Lawrence, Daniel Otero-Leon
- **NOFX相关度**: 0

## 摘要

We introduce the Double-Entropy Fit Index (DEFI), a regularized information-theoretic criterion for selecting among candidate graph partitions and reduced representations. DEFI couples entropy-based compression with relevance preservation and penalizes model dimensionality, cluster imbalance, and partition fragmentation. An exact Rényi-2 entropy identity shows that partitioning alters both spectral concentration and graph-volume normalization, explaining why unregularized entropy minimization can favor degenerate solutions. Across a 243-condition population-analytic factor recovery benchmark, DEFI achieved an overall exact recovery rate of 97.2\% (versus 75.0\% for EGA-TMFG, 45.8\% for TEFI on von Neumann entropy). In controlled local candidate batteries (distinguishing the true model from adjacent $K \pm 1$ and factor-split alternatives), DEFI identified the correct partition in 99.2\% of replications. Across ten clustering benchmarks, DEFI-guided Gaussian mixtures achieved a mean ARI of 0.929, exceeding HDBSCAN (0.809), Ward clustering (0.700), and conventional GMM criteria (0.501-0.580), with gains concentrating where standard criteria over-extract components.
