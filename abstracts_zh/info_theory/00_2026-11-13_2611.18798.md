# A proof of the Eskenazis--Nayar--Tkocz conjecture on entropy maximization for cube projections

- **arXiv**: [2611.18798](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18798)
- **日期**: 2026-11-13
- **分类**: info_theory
- **作者**: Vu Khac Ky
- **NOFX相关度**: 0

## 摘要

We complete the Eskenazis--Nayar--Tkocz conjecture: among the one-dimensional projections of the uniform measure on a cube, differential entropy is maximized precisely in directions with equal coordinate magnitudes. Pang proved the conjecture in every dimension at least $150$. We prove the remaining dimensions using polynomial entropy bounds in dimensions three through six and exact certificates for derivatives of the logarithm of the diagonal projection density. The proof uses integer arithmetic and certified one-dimensional integration. We also derive quantitative stability estimates and determine the optimal asymptotic coefficient of a linear stability bound. The source code and the outputs of all finite verifications accompany the paper.
