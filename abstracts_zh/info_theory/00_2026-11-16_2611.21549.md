# Axioms for relative entropy

- **arXiv**: [2611.21549](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21549)
- **日期**: 2026-11-16
- **分类**: info_theory
- **作者**: Roberto Longo
- **NOFX相关度**: 0

## 摘要

For normal states of arbitrary von Neumann algebras, we characterize positive finite multiples of Araki relative entropy among non-degenerate divergences. The axioms are monotonicity under normal unital completely positive maps, tensor additivity, superadditivity for product second states, and separate lower semicontinuity in the predual norm. Alternatively, a conditional-expectation identity replaces the two tensor assumptions. The proof uses order bounds and asymptotic estimates on tensor powers. An appendix proves the known analytic estimates needed here, using standard modular theory and Haagerup L^p spaces. We also obtain the corresponding characterization in the C* algebraic setting.
