# Exponential Analysis for Trace-Distance Errors in Quantum Information Theory

- **arXiv**: [2611.34468](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34468)
- **日期**: 2026-11-25
- **分类**: info_theory
- **作者**: Hao-Chung Cheng, Li Gao, Shi-Bing Li et al.
- **NOFX相关度**: 0

## 摘要

We study the trace-distance error in classical-quantum soft covering, privacy amplification against quantum side information, convex splitting, and decoupling by a Haar-random unitary followed by partial trace. In the independent and identically distributed setting, we determine exact reliability functions and strong converse exponents for soft covering with independent codewords, uniform convex splitting, and Haar partial-trace decoupling. For privacy amplification, we determine the exact reliability function for independent random binning and the exact strong converse exponent for every two-universal hash ensemble. The reliability and strong converse formulas involve sandwiched and Petz-Rényi information, respectively. The proofs connect trace-distance errors to quantum hypothesis testing, using Rosenthal inequalities for sums of random matrices in the reliability analysis and conditional second-moment bounds for the strong converses.
