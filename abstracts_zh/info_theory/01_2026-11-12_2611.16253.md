# A Constructive Theory of Closed-Form Rate-Distortion Functions

- **arXiv**: [2611.16253](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16253)
- **日期**: 2026-11-12
- **分类**: info_theory
- **作者**: Dazhuan Xu, Yongming Huang, Xiaohu You
- **NOFX相关度**: 1

## 摘要

Rate-distortion functions are defined by a variational problem whose analytic solution depends on the source distribution and distortion measure. We develop a posterior-based construction framework using conditional entropy as a common distortion coordinate. Building on the rate-entropy formulation, we establish entropy-level attainability for discrete sources with finite entropy and absolutely continuous sources with integrable log densities. We give a constructive rate-entropy coding proof for finite-alphabet sources and such continuous sources, without a second-moment assumption. Marginal compatibility ensures that a candidate posterior and output distribution recover the source, while a Gibbs representation and a kernel mass bound certify attainment of maximum conditional entropy. The negative log posterior identifies candidate fixed distortions. Excess rate equals a conditional entropy gap and decomposes into budget slack and posterior mismatch. Convolution decompositions and conjugate posteriors provide two construction paths. They recover established results, including the Gamma-source rate-distortion function under Itakura-Saito distortion, and yield a closed-form parametric expression for zeta sources under divisibility-constrained logarithmic multiplicative distortion. Exact expressions are obtained on specified intervals for negative binomial sources under one-sided counting distortion and Gamma sources under one-sided mean error. A residual-count coordinate for Poisson entropy budgets and rate-entropy parametrizations of Gamma and Dirichlet counting channels connect model parameters to remaining uncertainty. The framework unifies distortion selection, compatible-channel construction, and optimality verification.
