While the global statistic captures only the overall byte distribution, the local statistic is also sensitive to non-randomness that is con?ned to parts of the ciphertext. Both measures are commonly assessed against reference values derived under the assumption of independent uniform bytes. We derive the exact mean and variance of entropy under the valid-STL (VSTL) distribution, which assigns equal probability to every admissible sequence of triangle records, and provide second-order expansions that keep the global reference values practical to compute at large ?le sizes. Using these expansions, we establish a two-sided hypothesis test for the global entropy statistic, whose limiting value is lowered from 8 to 7.99991479609 bits.
