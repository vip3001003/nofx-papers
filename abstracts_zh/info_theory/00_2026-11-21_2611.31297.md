# Predictive Self-Entropy in Dirichlet Universal Coding: A Factor-Two Central Limit Theorem and a Markov Extension

- **arXiv**: [2611.31297](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31297)
- **日期**: 2026-11-21
- **分类**: info_theory
- **作者**: Thomas Schürmann
- **NOFX相关度**: 0

## 摘要

Sequential universal coders produce both a realized log-loss and, before each symbol is observed, a predictive distribution with its own Shannon entropy. We compare the realized arithmetic code length with the cumulative predictive self-entropy, the prequential sum of the self-expected logarithmic scores of the issued forecasts. This forecast-only quantity is neither a plug-in entropy estimate nor a posterior entropy functional. For finite-alphabet memoryless sources and Dirichlet (add-$α$) sequential predictors, we prove a joint central limit theorem for the two normalized quantities. They have the same first-order entropy limit but different second-order fluctuations: for every nonuniform i.i.d. source, cumulative predictive self-entropy has exactly twice the leading asymptotic variance of realized code length, and the ordinary mean-square-error ratio converges to two. We also give its logarithmic bias coefficient. For fixed finite-order Markov sources with context-wise Dirichlet predictors, the factor-two result becomes a context/innovation covariance decomposition, recovering the i.i.d. factor two when only the innovation component contributes.
