# K-out-of-N Oblivious Transfer over Erasure Channels: Capacity and Reductions

- **arXiv**: [2611.21359](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21359)
- **日期**: 2026-11-16
- **分类**: info_theory
- **作者**: Amirhossein Shekofteh, Rémi A. Chou
- **NOFX相关度**: 0

## 摘要

We study $K$-out-of-$N$ oblivious transfer (OT) between a server and a client over a discrete memoryless channel, under an honest-but-curious model. The client must retrieve $K$ out of $N$ messages stored by the server while learning nothing about the remaining $N-K$ messages, and the server must not learn the client's selection. For a binary erasure channel (BEC) with parameter $ε$, we show that the $K$-out-of-$N$ OT capacity is $\min\Big(\frac{Kε}{N-K},1-ε\Big)$. This characterization generalizes the known capacity results for $1$-out-of-$2$ OT and for $1$-out-of-$N$ OT when $ε\ge\tfrac{N-1}{N}$. We also investigate OT reductions, specifically, we show that constructing a $K$-out-of-$N$ OT protocol from repeating $K$ times an optimal $1$-out-of-$N$ OT protocol can be suboptimal, but we also identify certain ranges of $ε$ and $M < N$ for which a $1$-out-of-$N$ OT protocol can be optimally realized via the repetition of a $1$-out-of-$M$ OT protocol. Finally, we discuss extensions of our results to a generalized erasure channel (GEC).
