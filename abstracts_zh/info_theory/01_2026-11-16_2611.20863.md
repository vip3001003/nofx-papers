# Practical Semantic Communication for General-Purpose Networks: Compression of High-Dimensional Embeddings Under Quantization

- **arXiv**: [2611.20863](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.20863)
- **日期**: 2026-11-16
- **分类**: info_theory
- **作者**: Leonardo Roque, Louis-Adrien Dufrène, Quentin Lampin et al.
- **NOFX相关度**: 1

## 摘要

This paper introduces a practical framework for semantic communication over wireless channels, focusing on the compression of high-dimensional embeddings under quantization and channel errors. Building upon a relaxed Gaussian Information Bottleneck (GIB) model, the approach provides analytical solutions for task-relevant information preservation, even with non-Gaussian targets. The framework relaxes traditional Gaussian assumptions, enabling effective compression for discrete and complex data such as text embeddings. It derives closed-form gradients for optimizing the compression matrix and establishes bounds linking continuous representations to discrete, noisy system performance. Comprehensive experiments on text classification tasks demonstrate that the proposed method outperforms task-agnostic quantization-based compression methods, maintaining high semantic fidelity at low bit budgets per embedding. The approach offers a scalable, interpretable, and resource-efficient solution for deploying semantic communication in general-purpose networks.
